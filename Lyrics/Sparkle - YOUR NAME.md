# 🌌 1. Mada kono sekai wa

### まだこの世界は

🇬🇧 **Meaning:**  
**Even now, this world...**

### Breakdown

- **まだ (mada)** = still / yet
- **この (kono)** = this
- **世界 (sekai)** = world
- **は (wa)** = topic marker

The sentence continues into the next line.

---

# 2. Boku wo kainarashitetai mitai da

### 僕を飼い慣らしてたいみたいだ

🇬🇧 **Meaning:**  
**It seems this world still wants to tame me.**

### Breakdown

- **僕 (boku)** = I / me
- **を (o)** = object marker
- **飼い慣らす (kainarasu)** = tame / domesticate
- **～てたい** = want to keep doing
- **みたいだ (mitai da)** = seems like / appears to

### ⭐ 飼い慣らす

This literally means **tame an animal**.

So using it for a person/world is metaphorical:

> 世界が僕を飼い慣らす  
> = **the world tames/domesticates me**

It gives the feeling that society is trying to make him **fit into its rules**.

# 3. Nozomidoori darou?

### 望み通りだろう？

🇬🇧 **Meaning:**  
**Isn't this what I wanted?**

### Breakdown

- **望み (nozomi)** = wish / desire
- **通り (dōri)** = according to / as
- **望み通り** = just as desired
- **だろう** = probably / surely / isn't it?

### ⭐ ～通り

**N + 通り**

= **as / according to N**

> 予定通り  
> yotei dōri  
> = as planned

> 思った通り  
> omotta dōri  
> = just as I thought

So:

**望み通り**  
= **just as I wished**

---

# 4. Utsukushiku mogaku yo

### 美しくもがくよ

🇬🇧 **Meaning:**  
**I'll struggle beautifully.**

### Breakdown

- **美しい (utsukushii)** = beautiful
- **美しく (utsukushiku)** = beautifully
- **もがく (mogaku)** = struggle / writhe / desperately try
- **よ (yo)** = emphasis

### ⭐ い-adjective → く

This is an important grammar transformation:

> 美しい → 美しく  
> utsukushii → utsukushiku  
> beautiful → beautifully

> 強い → 強く  
> tsuyoi → tsuyoku  
> strong → strongly

So:

**美しくもがく**

= **struggle beautifully**

# 5. Tagai no sunadokei

### 互いの砂時計

🇬🇧 **Meaning:**  
**Our respective hourglasses / each other's hourglasses.**

### Breakdown

- **互い (tagai)** = each other / one another
- **の (no)** = of
- **砂時計 (sunadokei)** = hourglass

### Vocabulary:

**砂 (suna)** = sand  
**時計 (tokei)** = clock/watch

So:

> 砂時計  
> = **sand + clock = hourglass**

# 6. Nagamenagara kisu wo shiyou yo

### 眺めながらキスをしようよ

🇬🇧 **Meaning:**  
**Let's kiss while watching them.**

### Breakdown

- **眺める (nagameru)** = gaze at / look at
- **ながら (nagara)** = while doing
- **キスをする** = kiss
- **しよう** = let's do
- **よ** = emphasis

### ⭐ ～ながら

You saw this in the previous song too.

**Verb stem + ながら = while doing**

> 食べながら  
> tabenagara  
> = while eating

> 眺めながら  
> nagamenagara  
> = while gazing

So:

> 眺めながらキスをしよう  
> = **Let's kiss while watching.**

# 8. Basho de machiawaseyou

### 場所で待ち合わせよう

🇬🇧 **Meaning:**  
**Let's meet at that place.**

### Breakdown

- **場所 (basho)** = place
- **で (de)** = at
- **待ち合わせる (machiawaseru)** = meet up
- **待ち合わせよう** = let's meet

### ⭐ 待ち合わせる

This specifically means **meet up with someone**, usually at an agreed place/time.

> 駅で待ち合わせる

> = meet at the station

# 9. Jisho ni aru kotoba de dekiagatta sekai wo nikunda

### 辞書にある言葉で出来上がった世界を憎んだ

🇬🇧 **Meaning:**  
**I hated this world made from the words found in dictionaries.**

### Breakdown

- **辞書 (jisho)** = dictionary
- **にある** = that exist in / found in
- **言葉 (kotoba)** = words / language
- **出来上がる (dekiagaru)** = be completed / be made
- **世界 (sekai)** = world
- **憎む (nikumu)** = hate

### ⭐ で

Here:

> 言葉で出来上がった  
> kotoba de dekiagatta

= **made using words**

So the image is of a world constructed from the fixed definitions/words found in dictionaries.

# 11. Kimi wa boku no mae de hanikande wa sumashitemiseta

### 君は僕の前ではにかんでは澄ましてみせた

🇬🇧 **Meaning:**  
**You smiled shyly in front of me, then tried to look composed.**

### Breakdown

- **君 (kimi)** = you
- **僕 (boku)** = me
- **前 (mae)** = in front
- **はにかむ (hanikamu)** = smile shyly / be bashful
- **澄ます (sumasu)** = look composed / put on a calm expression
- **～てみせる** = do something to show that you can/will

### ⭐ ～てみせる

This is an interesting grammar pattern.

Literally:

> "do it and show you"

It can mean:

**"I'll do it / I'll show you that I can."**

Here **澄ましてみせた** has the feeling of:

> **tried to put on a composed face**

# 12. Kono sekai no kyoukasho no you na egao de

### この世界の教科書のような笑顔で

🇬🇧 **Meaning:**  
**With a smile like this world's textbook.**

This is poetic.

### Breakdown

- **この世界** = this world
- **教科書 (kyōkasho)** = textbook
- **ような (yō na)** = like / resembling
- **笑顔 (egao)** = smile / smiling face
- **で (de)** = with / by means of

### ⭐ Noun + のような + noun

= **a noun that is like another noun**

> 夢のような世界  
> yume no you na sekai  
> = a world like a dream

# 13. Tsui ni toki wa kita

🇬🇧 **Meaning:**

### Breakdown

- **ついに (tsui ni)** = finally / at last

- **来る (kuru)** = come

> **時が来た**

# 14. Kinou made wa joshou no joshou de

### 昨日までは序章の序章で

🇬🇧 **Meaning:**  
**Until yesterday, it was only the prologue of the prologue.**

### Breakdown

- **昨日 (kinō)** = yesterday
- **まで (made)** = until
- **序章 (joshō)** = prologue / opening chapter
- **序章の序章** = the prologue of the prologue

This is deliberately repetitive.

The idea is:

> **Everything before now was barely the beginning.**

# 15. Tobashiyomi de ii kara kokkara ga boku da yo

### 飛ばし読みでいいからここからが僕だよ

🇬🇧 **Meaning:**  
**It's okay to skim through it — from here on is where I become myself.**

### Breakdown

- **飛ばし読み (tobashiyomi)** = reading while skipping parts / skimming
- **でいい** = it's okay
- **から** = because / so
- **ここから** = from here
- **僕だ** = is me
- **よ** = emphasis

### ⭐ ここから

**ここ** = here  
**から** = from

> ここから  
> = **from here onward**

This is important because the song is marking a **turning point**.

# 16. Keiken to chishiki to kabi no haekakatta yuuki wo motte

### 経験と知識とカビの生えかかった勇気を持って

🇬🇧 **Meaning:**  
**Carrying experience, knowledge, and courage that has almost grown moldy.**

### Breakdown

- **経験 (keiken)** = experience
- **知識 (chishiki)** = knowledge
- **勇気 (yūki)** = courage
- **持つ (motsu)** = hold / have
- **カビが生える** = mold grows

### ⭐ カビの生えかかった

**カビ** = mold

**生える** = grow

So:

> カビの生えかかった勇気

literally:

> **"courage that has almost grown mold"**

Poetically, this means **old/stale courage that has been sitting unused for a long time**.

# 19. Koko de nai dokka wo yumemita yo

### ここでないどっかを夢見たよ

🇬🇧 **Meaning:**  
**I dreamed of somewhere that wasn't here.**

### Breakdown

- **ここ** = here
- **でない** = isn't / not
- **どっか** = somewhere (very casual)
- **夢見る (yumemiru)** = dream of

# 21. Densha ni yurare hakobareru asa ni

### 電車に揺られ運ばれる朝に

🇬🇧 **Meaning:**  
**On a morning carried along, swaying on the train.**

### Breakdown

- **電車 (densha)** = train
- **揺れる (yureru)** = sway
- **揺られる (yurareru)** = be swayed
- **運ぶ (hakobu)** = carry / transport
- **運ばれる (hakobareru)** = be carried
- **朝 (asa)** = morning

### ⭐ Passive:

**揺られる**

= **be swayed**

The train is swaying you around.

**運ばれる**

= **be carried / transported**

So the speaker isn't actively going somewhere — he is **being carried along by the train**.

# 22. Unmei da toka mirai toka tte kotoba ga dore dake te wo

### 運命だとか未来だとかって言葉がどれだけ手を...

🇬🇧 **Meaning:**  
**Words like "fate" and "future," no matter how much they...**

### Breakdown

- **運命 (unmei)** = fate / destiny
- **未来 (mirai)** = future
- **とか (toka)** = things like / such as
- **って (tte)** = casual quotation marker
- **言葉 (kotoba)** = words
- **どれだけ (dore dake)** = how much / no matter how much
- **手 (te)** = hand

The sentence continues.

### ⭐ AとかBとか

= **things like A and B**

> 日本とか韓国とか  
> Nihon toka Kankoku toka  
> = places like Japan and Korea

# 23. Nobasou to todokanai basho de bokura asobou ka

### 伸ばそうと届かない場所で僕ら遊ぼうか

🇬🇧 **Meaning:**  
**Shall we play in a place they can never reach, no matter how far they stretch their hands?**

### Breakdown

- **伸ばす (nobasu)** = stretch out
- **伸ばそう** = let's stretch / try to stretch
- **届く (todoku)** = reach
- **届かない** = cannot reach
- **場所 (basho)** = place
- **僕ら (bokura)** = we
- **遊ぼうか (asobou ka)** = shall we play?

### ⭐ ～ようか

**Verb volitional + か**

= **Shall we...?**

> 行こうか  
> ikou ka  
> = Shall we go?

> 食べようか  
> tabeyou ka  
> = Shall we eat?

> 遊ぼうか  
> asobou ka  
> = Shall we play?

# 27. Kono me ni yakitsuketeoku koto wa

### この目に焼き付けておくことは

🇬🇧 **Meaning:**  
**To burn it into these eyes / make sure I preserve it in my memory...**

### Breakdown

- **この目** = these eyes
- **焼き付ける (yakitsukeru)** = burn/imprint into
- **～ておく** = do in advance / keep done
- **こと** = act/fact of doing
- **は** = topic

### ⭐ 焼き付ける

Literally:

> 焼く = burn  
> 付ける = attach

But figuratively:

**焼き付ける = imprint deeply into one's memory**

So:

> 心に焼き付ける  
> kokoro ni yakitsukeru  
> = imprint deeply in one's heart

# 28. Mou kenri nanka janai gimu da to omounda

### もう権利なんかじゃない 義務だと思うんだ

🇬🇧 **Meaning:**  
**I don't think it's a right anymore — I think it's an obligation.**

### Breakdown

- **もう** = already / now
- **権利 (kenri)** = right
- **なんか** = things like / something like
- **じゃない** = is not
- **義務 (gimu)** = duty / obligation
- **と思う (to omou)** = think
- **んだ** = explanatory emphasis

### ⭐ なんか

**なんか** can make something sound dismissive:

> 権利なんか  
> = something like a right

Here:

> 権利なんかじゃない  
> = **It's not merely some "right."**

Instead:

> **義務だ**  
> = **it's a duty.**

### Breakdown

- **届く** = reach
- **届かない** = cannot reach
- **場所** = place
- **恋をする** = be in love / fall in love

# 🌌 The overall meaning

The song is basically about **breaking out of the world that has been defined for you and choosing your own future with someone important**.

The progression is really beautiful:

> **この世界は僕を飼い慣らしてたい**  
> _The world wants to tame me._

↓

> **ここからが僕だよ**  
> _From here on, this is me._

↓

> **君のもとへダイブを**  
> _I'll dive toward you._

↓

> **君のすべてをこの目に焼き付けておく**  
> _I'll imprint everything about you into my eyes._

↓

> **権利なんかじゃない 義務だ**  
> _It's not merely a right; it's a duty._

↓

> **生き抜いてゆこう**  
> _Let's live through it all._

That **ここからが僕だよ** is especially important.

**ここから** = from here  
**が** = marks the subject/focus  
**僕** = me  
**だよ** = it is / emphatic

So the emotional meaning is:

> **"Everything before this was only the prologue. From this point onward, this is my story."**

And the final **生き抜いてゆこう** isn't simply "let's live."

**生き抜く** means **to make it through, to survive, to live all the way through something difficult**.

So the ending is much stronger:

> **"No matter how many lifetimes it takes, let's make it through this world together."**
