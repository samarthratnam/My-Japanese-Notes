## 🎵 Intro

### 羽ばたいたら — Habataitara

> **Once I spread my wings / Once I take flight**

- **羽ばたく (habataku)** — to flap one's wings, to take flight
    
- **～たら (tara)** — if / when / once
    

### 戻らないと言って — Modoranai to itte

> **Say that I won't return.**

- **戻る (modoru)** — to return
    
- **～ない (-nai)** — not
    
- **と言って (to itte)** — saying / say that...
    

### 目指したのは — Mezashita no wa

> **What I aimed for was...**

- **目指す (mezasu)** — to aim for
    
- **のは (no wa)** — nominalizes the preceding phrase and sets it as the topic
    

### 青い青いあの空 — Aoi aoi ano sora

> **That blue, blue sky.**

- **青い (aoi)** — blue
    
- **あの (ano)** — that
    
- **空 (sora)** — sky
    

### 🌱 Natural meaning

> **“Once I take flight, I say I won't return.  
> What I aimed for was that blue, blue sky.”**

# 🎼 Verse 1

### 「悲しみ」はまだ覚えられず — “Kanashimi” wa mada oboerarezu

> **I still haven't learned what sadness is.**

- **悲しみ (kanashimi)** — sadness
    
- **まだ (mada)** — still / yet
    
- **覚える (oboeru)** — to remember / learn
    
- **～られず (-rarezu)** — without being able to / unable to
    

### 「切なさ」は今つかみ始めた — “Setsunasa” wa ima tsukami hajimeta

> **But now I've begun to grasp what sorrow feels like.**

- **切なさ (setsunasa)** — sadness, ache, bittersweet pain
    
- **今 (ima)** — now
    
- **つかむ (tsukamu)** — to grasp
    
- **～始める (-hajimeru)** — to begin doing something
    

### あなたへと抱くこの感情も — Anata e to idaku kono kanjou mo

> **Even these feelings I hold toward you...**

- **あなた (anata)** — you
    
- **抱く (idaku)** — to hold / embrace / harbor
    
- **この (kono)** — this
    
- **感情 (kanjou)** — emotion / feeling
    
- **も (mo)** — also / even
    

### 今「言葉」に変わっていく — Ima “kotoba” ni kawatte iku

> **Are now beginning to turn into words.**

- **言葉 (kotoba)** — word(s) / language
    
- **変わる (kawaru)** — to change / become
    
- **～ていく (-te iku)** — continue changing / gradually become
    

### 🌱 Natural meaning

> **“I still don't know sadness,  
> but now I'm beginning to understand sorrow.  
> Even these feelings I hold for you  
> are slowly turning into words.”**

# 🎼 Verse 2

### 愛想尽きたような音で — Aisou tsukita you na oto de

> **With a sound that seems completely weary / fed up...**

- **愛想 (aiso)** — friendliness / affection
    
- **愛想が尽きる (aiso ga tsukiru)** — to become fed up with someone/something
    
- **ような (you na)** — seeming like / as if
    
- **音 (oto)** — sound
    

### 錆びれた古い窓は壊れた — Sabireta furui mado wa kowareta

> **The old, rusted window broke.**

- **錆びれた (sabireta)** — rusted / decayed
    
- **古い (furui)** — old
    
- **窓 (mado)** — window
    
- **壊れる (kowareru)** — to break
    

### 見飽きた籠はほら捨てていく — Miakita kago wa hora sutete iku

> **Look, I'm leaving behind the cage I've grown tired of.**

- **見飽きる (miakiru)** — to become tired of seeing
    
- **籠 (kago)** — cage / basket
    
- **ほら (hora)** — look / see
    
- **捨てる (suteru)** — to throw away / abandon
    
- **～ていく (-te iku)** — go on doing / leave behind while moving forward
    

### 振り返ることはもうない — Furikaeru koto wa mou nai

> **I won't look back anymore.**

- **振り返る (furikaeru)** — to look back
    
- **こと (koto)** — nominalizer; turns a verb phrase into a noun-like phrase
    
- **もうない (mou nai)** — no longer / not anymore
    

### 🪶 Metaphor

The **cage** represents being trapped or confined.

**Breaking the window → leaving the cage → spreading wings → taking flight**

This represents **breaking free and moving forward**.

# 🌌 What the Song Means

The song isn't really just about **a bird flying**.

The bird is a metaphor for:

> **Breaking free from your old self and pursuing something beyond your current limits.**

### The imagery

```
Cage
  ↓
Window
  ↓
Wings
  ↓
Clouds
  ↓
Blue Sky
```

The progression represents:

- **籠 (kago) — Cage** → feeling trapped
    
- **窓 (mado) — Window** → an opening / opportunity
    
- **羽 (hane) — Wings** → freedom and ability
    
- **雲 (kumo) — Clouds** → a goal to reach
    
- **空 (sora) — Sky** → freedom / an open future
    
- **光 (hikari) — Light** → something worth pursuing
    

### 💭 Core idea

> **“I'm leaving the place that confined me.  
> I don't know what awaits me.  
> I may fall, and I'm afraid.  
> But I'm going to spread my wings anyway  
> and keep chasing that blue sky.”**

The repeated **青い青いあの空 (aoi aoi ano sora)** — _that blue, blue sky_ — represents an open future, freedom, and something you desperately want to reach.

# 📚 Grammar to Learn

- **～たら** → if / when / once
    
- **～ない** → negative form
    
- **～始める** → begin doing
    
- **～ていく** → continue/change going forward
    
- **～すぎる** → too much / excessively
    
- **～続ける** → continue doing
    
- **～ほど** → to the extent that / the more...
    
- **～と言って** → say that...
    
- **～こと** → nominalizer
    
- **それでも** → even so / nevertheless

> **羽ばたく (habataku)** → spread your wings  
> **飛び立つ (tobitatsu)** → take flight  
> **振り返る (furikaeru)** → look back  
> **追い続ける (oitsuzukeru)** → keep chasing  
> **光 (hikari)** → light  
> **青い空 (aoi sora)** → blue sky

**Blue Bird = freedom, leaving the cage, accepting the possibility of falling, and continuing toward the future anyway.**
