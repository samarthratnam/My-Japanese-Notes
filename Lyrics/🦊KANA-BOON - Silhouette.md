# 🎵 Verse 1

## 1. Isse no se de fumikomu goorain bokura wa

**一斉のせで踏み込むゴールライン 僕らは**

🇬🇧 **Meaning:**  
**With one, two, three, we step across the goal line — we...**

### Breakdown

- **一斉 (issei)** = all at once / together
    
- **のせで** = on the cue/count of "ready, set..." — likely a lyrical expression
    
- **踏み込む (fumikomu)** = step into / step forward / step across
    
- **ゴールライン (gōru rain)** = goal line
    
- **僕ら (bokura)** = we / us
    

### Vocabulary:

**踏む (fumu)** = step on

**踏み込む (fumikomu)** = step into / enter deeply

> 部屋に踏み込む  
> _heya ni fumikomu_  
> = step into a room

---

## 2. Nanimo nanimo mada shiranu

**何も何もまだ知らぬ**

🇬🇧 **Meaning:**  
**We still know nothing, nothing at all.**

### Breakdown

- **何も (nanimo)** = anything/nothing
    
- **まだ (mada)** = still / yet
    
- **知る (shiru)** = know
    
- **知らぬ (shiranu)** = do not know
    

### ⭐ Important:

**知らぬ** is an older/literary-sounding negative form of:

> 知らない  
> **shiranai** = don't know

So:

> まだ知らぬ  
> **mada shiranu**  
> = still don't know

In normal modern conversation you'd normally hear:

> まだ知らない

---

# 3. Issen koete furikaeruto mou nai bokura wa

**一線越えて振り返るともうない僕らは**

🇬🇧 **Meaning:**  
**When we cross that line and look back, it's already gone — we...**

### Breakdown

- **一線 (issen)** = a line / boundary
    
- **越える (koeru)** = cross / go beyond
    
- **振り返る (furikaeru)** = look back / turn around
    
- **もうない (mō nai)** = is no longer there / is already gone
    
- **僕ら (bokura)** = we
    

### ⭐ Important:

**振り返る**

> 振る = swing/turn  
> 返る = return

Together:

**振り返る = look back**

You'll hear this a lot.

# 9. Daiji ni shitetta mono

# 10. Wasureta furi o shitanda yo

**忘れたふりをしたんだよ**

🇬🇧 **Meaning:**  
**We pretended we'd forgotten.**

### Breakdown

- **忘れる (wasureru)** = forget
    
- **忘れた (wasureta)** = forgot
    
- **ふりをする (furi o suru)** = pretend
    
- **した (shita)** = did
    
- **んだよ (n da yo)** = explanatory + emphasis
    

### ⭐ VERY useful grammar:

**～ふりをする**

= **pretend to ～**

> 知らないふりをする  
> _shiranai furi o suru_  
> = pretend not to know

> 寝たふりをする  
> _neta furi o suru_  
> = pretend to be asleep

Here:

> 忘れたふりをした  
> = **pretended to have forgotten**

# 🎵 Verse 2

## 12. Isse no de, omoidasu shounen

**一斉ので、思い出す少年**

The supplied romaji appears slightly off here.

Important words:

- **思い出す (omoidasu)** = remember / recall
    
- **少年 (shōnen)** = boy / young boy
    

### ⭐ 思い出す

This is different from **覚える**.

**覚える** = remember / memorize something

**思い出す** = **recall / remember something that had been forgotten**

For example:

> 名前を思い出した。  
> _Namae o omoidasita._  
> = I remembered his name.

# 14. Wakat teru tte, aa kizuiteru tte

**分かってるって、ああ気づいてるって**

🇬🇧 **Meaning:**  
**"I know, I know... Ah, I realize it, I do."**

### Breakdown

- **分かってる (wakatte iru)** = I know / I understand
    
- **って (tte)** = casual quotation/emphasis
    
- **気づいてる (kizuite iru)** = have realized / am aware
    

### ⭐ ～って

**って** is extremely common in casual Japanese.

It can replace:

> と言って  
> と言う  
> と

depending on context.

Here it has the feeling of:

> **"I know, I'm telling you!"**

# 🌱 The main idea of the song

The lyrics move through a really nice progression:

**少年 → 何も知らない → 何もかも欲しがる → 時間が流れる → 記憶が遠くなる → 大人になる → 大事なものを守り続ける.**

In simple English:

> **We started out knowing nothing. We wanted everything. Time kept moving, taking memories with it. Some things became distant and some things were forgotten. But someone taught us that there are things that don't change. So, as we grow into adults, we'll hold onto and protect the things we cherish — until someday we can look back and laugh about everything.**

And the imagery at the end is important:

**木の葉が飛んでゆく**  
_The leaves fly away._

It mirrors the earlier idea of:

**記憶 → 遠く遠く遠くになって**  
_memories becoming farther and farther away._

But instead of ending with only loss, the song ends with **protecting what matters and continuing forward**.
