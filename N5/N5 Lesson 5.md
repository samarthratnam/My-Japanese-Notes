# Lesson 5 – Grammar

---

# I. Direction Particles

### Structure

**N (Place) + へ (e) + Ikimasu / Kimasu / Kaerimasu**

> The particle **へ** is pronounced **"e"**.

Meaning

> Go / Come / Return to a place.

Examples

```
Watashi wa gakkō e ikimasu.
I go to school.

Tomodachi wa uchi e kimasu.
My friend comes home.

Watashi wa ie e kaerimasu.
I return home.
```

More Examples

```
Mainichi daigaku e ikimasu.
I go to university every day.

Kyōshitsu e kimasu.
Come to the classroom.

Ashita uchi e kaerimasu.
I will return home tomorrow.
```

---

# II. Means of Transportation

### Structure

**Place + へ + Transportation + で + Ikimasu / Kimasu / Kaerimasu**

Meaning

> Go / Come / Return by a means of transportation.

Examples

```
Gakkō e jitensha de ikimasu.
I go to school by bicycle.

Tōkyō e shinkansen de ikimasu.
I go to Tokyo by Shinkansen.

Kaisha e basu de ikimasu.
I go to the company by bus.
```

More Examples

```
Depāto e takushī de ikimasu.
I go to the department store by taxi.

Kūkō e densha de ikimasu.
I go to the airport by train.

Daigaku e chikatetsu de ikimasu.
I go to the university by subway.
```

---

# III. With Whom?

### Structure

**Person / Animal + と (to) + Verb**

Meaning

> Do an action with someone.

Examples

```
Tomodachi to gakkō e ikimasu.
I go to school with my friend.

Kazoku to ryokō shimasu.
I travel with my family.

Sensei to hanashimasu.
I talk with my teacher.
```

More Examples

```
Haha to depāto e ikimasu.
I go to the department store with my mother.

Inu to kōen e ikimasu.
I go to the park with my dog.
```

---

# IV. Going Alone

### Structure

**Hitori de + Verb**

Meaning

> Do something alone.

Examples

```
Hitori de gakkō e ikimasu.
I go to school alone.

Hitori de basu de kaerimasu.
I return by bus alone.

Hitori de tabemasu.
I eat alone.
```

---

# V. Asking "When?"

### Structure

**Itsu + Verb?**

Meaning

> When do you...?

Examples

```
Itsu Nihon e ikimasu ka?
When will you go to Japan?

Itsu kaerimasu ka?
When will you return?

Itsu benkyō shimasu ka?
When do you study?
```

Answers

```
Raishū ikimasu.
I will go next week.

Ashita kaerimasu.
I will return tomorrow.

Maiasa benkyō shimasu.
I study every morning.
```

> **Correction:** **Itsu** does **not** take the particle **に**.

Correct:

- Itsu ikimasu ka?

Not:

- ❌ Itsu ni ikimasu ka?

---

# VI. Sentence Ending 「よ」

The particle **よ (yo)** adds emphasis or gives new information.

Examples

```
Kore wa omoshiroi desu yo.
This is interesting!

Ashita yasumi desu yo.
Tomorrow is a holiday!

Koko wa gakkō desu yo.
This is the school.
```
