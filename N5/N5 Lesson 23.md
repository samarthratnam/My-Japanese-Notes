# SECTION 2: 🧠 Grammar

## 1. ～とき (時)

**Meaning / Use:**  
Used to say **“when”** or **“at the time when”** something happens.

**Structure:**

```text
Verb + とき
い-adjective + とき
な-adjective + な + とき
Noun + の + とき
```

**Explanation:**

- The form before **とき** depends on the word before it.
    
- Verb: use the appropriate form.
    
- い-adjective: use the normal form.
    
- な-adjective: add **な**.
    
- Noun: add **の**.
    

**Examples:**

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|食べるとき|taberu toki|when eating|
|暑いとき|atsui toki|when it is hot|
|暇なとき|hima na toki|when I am free|
|子供のとき|kodomo no toki|when I was a child|

**Common mistakes:**

- ❌ 食べますとき → ✅ 食べるとき
    
- ❌ 暇とき → ✅ 暇なとき
    

---

## 2. V-dictionary / Vた + とき

**Meaning / Use:**  
The verb form before **とき** shows whether the action is **not yet completed** or **completed**.

**Structure:**

```text
V-dictionary + とき → action not completed
Vた + とき → action completed
```

**Explanation:**

- **帰るとき** = when going back / before completing the return.
    
- **帰ったとき** = when/after returning.
    
- Dictionary form describes an action that has not yet been completed.
    
- た-form describes an action that has been completed.
    

**Examples:**

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|国へ帰るとき、かばんを買いました。|Kuni e kaeru toki, kaban o kaimashita.|I bought a bag when I was going back to my country.|
|国へ帰ったとき、かばんを買いました。|Kuni e kaetta toki, kaban o kaimashita.|I bought a bag when I returned to my country.|
|若いとき、あまり勉強しませんでした。|Wakai toki, amari benkyō shimasen deshita.|When I was young, I did not study much.|
|子供のとき、川で泳ぎました。|Kodomo no toki, kawa de oyogimashita.|When I was a child, I swam in a river.|

**Common mistakes:**

- **帰るとき** and **帰ったとき** do not have exactly the same meaning.
    
- Remember: **dictionary = not completed**, **た-form = completed**.
    

---

## 3. Vない + とき

**Meaning / Use:**  
Used to say **“when not doing...”** or **“when you don't...”**.

**Structure:**

```text
V-nai + とき
```

**Explanation:**

- Change the verb to its **ない-form**.
    
- Then add **とき**.
    
- It describes a situation where the action is not done.
    
- Example: **分からないとき** = when you don't know / understand.
    

**Examples:**

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|分からないとき、私に聞いてください。|Wakaranai toki, watashi ni kiite kudasai.|When you don't know, ask me.|
|行かないとき|ikanai toki|when you don't go|
|食べないとき|tabenai toki|when you don't eat|
|分からないとき|wakaranai toki|when you don't understand / know|

**Common mistakes:**

- ❌ 分かりますとき → ✅ 分からないとき when saying “when you don't know.”
    

---

## 4. V-dictionary + と

**Meaning / Use:**  
Used when **A naturally or automatically causes B**.

**Structure:**

```text
V-dictionary + と + natural / automatic result
```

**Explanation:**

- The result happens naturally or automatically.
    
- Common with machines, instructions, directions, and natural consequences.
    
- It can describe **“when/if A happens, B happens.”**
    
- Intention, hope, invitation, and request normally do not follow this pattern.
    

**Examples:**

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|このボタンを押すと、おつりが出ます。|Kono botan o osu to, otsuri ga demasu.|When you press this button, change comes out.|
|これを回すと、音が大きくなります。|Kore o mawasu to, oto ga ōkiku narimasu.|When you turn this, the sound becomes louder.|
|右へ曲がると、郵便局があります。|Migi e magaru to, yūbinkyoku ga arimasu.|Turn right and you will find a post office.|
|このボタンを押すと、切手が出ます。|Kono botan o osu to, kitte ga demasu.|When you press this button, a ticket comes out.|

**Common mistakes:**

- ❌ 時間があると、映画を見に行きます。
    
- ✅ 時間があったら、映画を見に行きます。  
    Personal intention is expressed with **たら** here.

## 5. N が + Adjective / Verb

**Meaning / Use:**  
**が** marks the noun being described in a state or scene.

**Structure:**

```text
N + が + adjective
N + が + verb
```

**Explanation:**

- The noun being described is marked with **が**.
    
- **N が + adjective** describes the state of N.
    
- **N が + verb** describes what N does or what is happening.
    
- This is useful when describing what is happening in a scene.
    

**Examples:**

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|音が小さいです。|Oto ga chiisai desu.|The sound is low.|
|電気が明るくなりました。|Denki ga akaruku narimashita.|The light became brighter.|
|切手が出ます。|Kitte ga demasu.|A ticket comes out.|
|おつりが出ます。|Otsuri ga demasu.|Change comes out.|

**Common mistakes:**

- Remember that **が** marks the noun being described in this pattern.
    

---

## 6. N(place) を + Movement Verb

**Meaning / Use:**  
Here **を** marks the **place or path through which someone moves**, rather than a normal object.

**Structure:**

```text
N(place) + を + movement verb
```

**Explanation:**

- Used with movement verbs such as **歩きます, 渡ります, 散歩します**.
    
- **道を歩きます** = walk along the road.
    
- **橋を渡ります** = cross the bridge.
    
- Here **を** marks the path/place through which movement occurs.
    

**Examples:**

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|公園を散歩します。|Kōen o sanpo shimasu.|I take a walk in/through the park.|
|道を歩きます。|Michi o arukimasu.|I walk along the road.|
|橋を渡ります。|Hashi o watarimasu.|I cross the bridge.|
|交差点を右へ曲がります。|Kōsaten o migi e magarimasu.|I turn right at the intersection.|

**Common mistakes:**

- Do not think of **を** here only as a normal object marker.
    
- **Place/path を + movement verb** = move through/along the place.
    

---

## 7. ～とき: Adjective + とき

**Meaning / Use:**  
Used to describe **when something has a particular quality or state**.

**Structure:**

```text
い-adjective + とき
な-adjective + な + とき
```

**Explanation:**

- い-adjective uses its normal form.
    
- な-adjective needs **な** before **とき**.
    
- Example: **暑いとき** = when it is hot.
    
- Example: **暇なとき** = when I am free.
    

**Examples:**

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|暑いとき|atsui toki|when it is hot|
|体の調子が悪いとき、元気茶を飲みます。|Karada no chōshi ga warui toki, Genki-cha o nomimasu.|When I am not in good condition, I drink Genki-cha.|
|暇なとき、うちへ遊びに来ませんか。|Hima na toki, uchi e asobi ni kimasen ka?|Won't you come to my place when you are free?|
|寒いとき|samui toki|when it is cold|

**Common mistakes:**

- ❌ 暇とき
    
- ✅ 暇なとき
    

---

## 8. N + の + とき

**Meaning / Use:**  
Used to say **“when I was/were N.”**

**Structure:**

```text
Noun + の + とき
```

**Explanation:**

- A noun needs **の** before **とき**.
    
- **子供のとき** = when I was a child.
    
- **学生のとき** = when I was a student.
    
- Use the **N + の + とき** structure.
    

**Examples:**

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|私が子供のとき、よく川で泳ぎました。|Watashi ga kodomo no toki, yoku kawa de oyogimashita.|When I was a child, I often swam in a river.|
|学生のとき|Gakusei no toki|when I was a student|
|子供のとき|Kodomo no toki|when I was a child|
|日本人のとき|Nihonjin no toki|when [someone] was Japanese|

**Common mistakes:**

- ❌ 子供とき
    
- ✅ 子供のとき

## 9. ～と vs ～たら

**Meaning / Use:**  
**～と** describes a natural or automatic result, while **～たら** is used when the result involves intention, hope, invitation, or request.

**Structure:**

```text
V-dictionary + と + automatic result
Vたら + result involving intention / hope / invitation / request
```

**Explanation:**

- **～と** → natural / automatic result.
    
- **～たら** → personal decision, hope, invitation, or request.
    
- **ボタンを押すと** → the result happens naturally.
    
- **時間があったら** → personal decision or intention.
    

**Examples:**

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|ボタンを押すと、音が出ます。|Botan o osu to, oto ga demasu.|When you press the button, sound comes out.|
|このボタンを押すと、おつりが出ます。|Kono botan o osu to, otsuri ga demasu.|When you press this button, change comes out.|
|時間があったら、映画を見に行きます。|Jikan ga attara, eiga o mi ni ikimasu.|If I have time, I will go see a movie.|
|暇なとき、うちへ遊びに来ませんか。|Hima na toki, uchi e asobi ni kimasen ka?|Won't you come to my place when you are free?|

**Common mistakes:**

- ❌ Using **～と** for a personal decision.
    
- Use **～たら** when the result expresses intention, hope, invitation, or request.
