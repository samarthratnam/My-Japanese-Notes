# SECTION 2: 🧠 GRAMMAR

## 1. ～たら — If / When / After

### ～たら

**Meaning / Use:**  
Used for **if**, **when**, or **after** something happens.

**Structure:**

> **Plain past form + ら**

> Vた + ら

### Explanation

- Make the **た-form** first.
    
- Add **ら** to the た-form.
    
- Even though it uses the past form, it can describe a future condition.
    
- It can mean **if**, **when**, or **after**, depending on context.
    
- Basic idea: **Condition + たら, result.**
    

### Formation

|Basic form|た-form|～たら|Romaji|
|---|---|---|---|
|行く|行った|行ったら|ittara|
|食べる|食べた|食べたら|tabetara|
|飲む|飲んだ|飲んだら|nondara|
|する|した|したら|shitara|
|来る|来た|来たら|kitara|

### Examples

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|お金があったら、旅行します。|Okane ga attara, ryokou shimasu.|If I had money, I would travel.|
|時間がなかったら、テレビを見ません。|Jikan ga nakattara, terebi o mimasen.|If I don't have time, I won't watch TV.|
|安かったら、パソコンを買いたいです。|Yasukattara, pasokon o kaitai desu.|If it is inexpensive, I want to buy a personal computer.|
|暇だったら、手伝ってください。|Hima dattara, tetsudatte kudasai.|If you are free, please give me a hand.|

### Common mistakes

**ない → なかった → なかったら**

> お金があったら。✅  
> お金があった。❌

## 2. ～たら — When / After

### Vた + ら

**Meaning / Use:**  
Used to say **when / after** something has happened.

**Structure:**

> **Vた + ら, main action**

### Explanation

- The action/state in the **たら clause happens first**.
    
- The main action happens after it.
    
- Context tells you whether ～たら means **if**, **when**, or **after**.
    
- Timeline: **first action → main action**.
    

### Examples

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|十時になったら、出かけましょう。|Juu-ji ni nattara, dekakemashou.|Let's go out when it gets to ten.|
|家へ帰ったら、すぐシャワーを浴びます。|Uchi e kaettara, sugu shawaa o abimasu.|I take a shower soon after I return home.|
|友達が来る前に、部屋を掃除します。|Tomodachi ga kuru mae ni, heya o souji shimasu.|I will clean my room before my friends come.|
|友達が来なかったら、どうしますか。|Tomodachi ga konakattara, dou shimasu ka?|If your friend doesn't come, what will you do?|

### Common mistakes

Do not always translate **～たら** as only “if.”

> ～たら = **if / when / after**

---

## 3. ～ても — Even If

### ～ても

**Meaning / Use:**  
Used for a **reverse condition**: even if A happens, B still happens.

**Structure:**

> **Vて + も**

> **い-adjective → ～くても**

> **な-adjective → ～でも**

> **Noun + でも**

### Explanation

- Verb: **Vて + も**
    
- い-adjective: change **い → くても**
    
- な-adjective: use **でも**
    
- Noun: use **N + でも**
    
- The result does not change even when the condition happens.
    

### Examples

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|雨が降っても、洗濯します。|Ame ga futte mo, sentaku shimasu.|Even if it rains, I will do the laundry.|
|安くても、私はグループ旅行が嫌いです。|Yasukute mo, watashi wa guruupu ryokou ga kirai desu.|Even if group tours are inexpensive, I don't like them.|
|便利でも、パソコンを使いません。|Benri demo, pasokon o tsukaimasen.|Even if a personal computer is useful, I won't use it.|
|日曜日でも、働きます。|Nichiyoubi demo, hatarakimasu.|Even if it is Sunday, I will work.|

### Common mistakes

> 安い → 安くても ✅  
> 安いても ❌

---

## 4. もし — If / Hypothetically

### もし + ～たら

**Meaning / Use:**  
**もし** emphasizes the conditional idea and is commonly used with **～たら**.

**Structure:**

> **もし + condition + たら, result**

### Explanation

- もし means **if**.
    
- It emphasizes the condition.
    
- It can give the feeling of **“if, hypothetically...”**
    
- もし is not always necessary.
    

### Examples

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|もしお金があったら、旅行したいです。|Moshi okane ga attara, ryokou shitai desu.|If I had money, I would like to travel.|
|もし一億円あったら、いろいろな国を旅行したいです。|Moshi ichi oku en attara, iroiro na kuni o ryokou shitai desu.|If I had 100 million yen, I would like to travel to various countries.|
|お金があったら、旅行したいです。|Okane ga attara, ryokou shitai desu.|If I had money, I would like to travel.|
|もしお金があったら、旅行したいです。|Moshi okane ga attara, ryokou shitai desu.|If I had money, I would like to travel.|

### Common mistakes

**もし** is not required every time.

> お金があったら... ✅  
> もしお金があったら... ✅

---

## 5. いくら～ても — No Matter How / However Much

### いくら～ても

**Meaning / Use:**  
Used to say **no matter how much** or **however**.

**Structure:**

> **いくら + ～ても**

### Explanation

- いくら means **how much**.
    
- Combined with ～ても, it means **no matter how much / however**.
    
- The condition does not change the result.
    
- It can be used with verbs and adjectives.
    

### Examples

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|いくら考えても、分かりません。|Ikura kangaete mo, wakarimasen.|No matter how much I think, I can't understand it.|
|いくら高くても、買います。|Ikura takakute mo, kaimasu.|No matter how expensive it is, I will buy it.|
|高くても、買います。|Takakute mo, kaimasu.|Even if it is expensive, I will buy it.|
|いくら高くても、買います。|Ikura takakute mo, kaimasu.|No matter how expensive it is, I will buy it.|

### Common mistakes

> 高くても = even if it is expensive  
> いくら高くても = no matter how expensive it is

## 6. N が in Subordinate Clauses

### N が + Clause

**Meaning / Use:**  
In a subordinate clause, **が** can mark the subject of the smaller clause.

**Structure:**

> **N が + clause**

### Explanation

- が identifies the subject of the smaller clause.
    
- The smaller clause is inside the larger sentence.
    
- Example: **友達が来る** = my friend comes.
    
- The subject is **友達**, marked by **が**.
    
- This is especially seen inside clauses such as ～たら.
    

### Examples

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|友達が来る前に、部屋を掃除します。|Tomodachi ga kuru mae ni, heya o souji shimasu.|I will clean my room before my friends come.|
|妻が病気の時、会社を休みます。|Tsuma ga byouki no toki, kaisha o yasumimasu.|When my wife is sick, I take a day off from work.|
|友達が約束の時間に来なかったら、どうしますか。|Tomodachi ga yakusoku no jikan ni konakattara, dou shimasu ka?|If your friend doesn't come on time, what will you do?|
|子供の時|Kodomo no toki|when I was a child|

### Common mistakes

Inside the smaller clause:

> 友達が来る ✅  
> **が** marks the subject of the smaller clause.

# 🔄 ～たら vs ～ても

|Grammar|Meaning|Example|
|---|---|---|
|～たら|if / when|雨が降ったら...|
|～ても|even if|雨が降っても...|

### Easy memory

> **たら = IF / WHEN**  
> **ても = EVEN IF**

# 📝 Quick Formula Sheet

```text
IF / WHEN / AFTER

V-ta + ra
→ if V / when V / after V

Example:
食べたら
tabetara
→ if/when I eat


EVEN IF

V-te + mo
→ even if V

Example:
食べても
tabete mo
→ even if I eat


I-ADJECTIVE

～くても
→ even if...

Example:
高くても
takakute mo
→ even if expensive


NA-ADJECTIVE

～でも
→ even if...

Example:
暇でも
hima demo
→ even if free


NOUN

N + でも
→ even if N...


HYPOTHETICAL

もし + condition
→ if...


NO MATTER HOW

いくら + ～ても
→ no matter how...


SUBORDINATE SUBJECT

N が + clause
→ N is the subject of the smaller clause
```
