# JLPT N5 Japanese Grammar Formula Master List — Lessons 1–25

**Format:** formula → use → example (romaji) → English.  
**Important:** Particles are listed again wherever they perform a different job in another formula. Do not treat a particle as having only one meaning.

## Lesson 1 — Basic sentences

1. **N1 は N2 です。** — N1 is N2.  
   *Watashi wa gakusei desu.* — I am a student.
2. **N1 は N2 じゃありません。** — N1 is not N2.  
   *Watashi wa sensei ja arimasen.* — I am not a teacher.
3. **N1 は N2 ですか。** — Is N1 N2?  
   *Anata wa gakusei desu ka?* — Are you a student?
4. **N1 も N2 です。** — N1 is also N2.  
   *Watashi mo gakusei desu.* — I am also a student.
5. **N1 は N2 です。か** — Question marker **か** turns a statement into a question.  
   *Tanaka-san wa sensei desu ka?* — Is Mr./Ms. Tanaka a teacher?
6. **Place / country から 来ました。** — Came from a place/country.  
   *Indo kara kimashita.* — I came from India.
7. **こちらは N です。** — This is N (introducing someone).  
   *Kochira wa Tanaka-san desu.* — This is Mr./Ms. Tanaka.

## Lesson 2 — Demonstratives and の

8. **これ／それ／あれ は N です。** — This/that/that over there is N.  
   *Kore wa hon desu.* — This is a book.
9. **この／その／あの + N** — This/that/that over there + noun.  
   *Kono hon wa watashi no desu.* — This book is mine.
10. **N1 の N2** — N2 belonging to, associated with, or describing N1.  
    *Watashi no hon.* — My book.
11. **S1 か S2 か。** — S1 or S2?  
    *Kore wa hon desu ka, zasshi desu ka?* — Is this a book or a magazine?
12. **N ですか。→ はい、そうです／いいえ、そうじゃありません。** — Confirm or deny.  
    *Sore wa jisho desu ka? Hai, sou desu.* — Is that a dictionary? Yes, it is.

## Lesson 3 — Places and location questions

13. **N1 は N2 (place) です。** — N1 is at/in/on a place.  
    *Jimusho wa ni-kai desu.* — The office is on the second floor.
14. **N は どこですか。** — Where is N?  
    *Toire wa doko desu ka?* — Where is the toilet?
15. **N は どちらですか。** — Where/which way is N? (polite).  
    *Uketsuke wa dochira desu ka?* — Where is reception?
16. **N1 の N2** — Possession, origin, type, or association.  
    *Nihon no kuruma.* — A Japanese car.
17. **お + selected noun** — Polite/respectful prefix for certain words.  
    *Onamae wa?* — Your name? (polite)

## Lesson 4 — Time and verb forms

18. **Specific time + に + Vます。** — Do an action at a specific time.  
    *Roku-ji ni okimasu.* — I get up at six.
19. **Vます → Vません。** — Polite non-past negative.  
    *Ashita hatarakimasen.* — I will not work tomorrow.
20. **Vますか。** — Ask whether someone does/will do something.  
    *Mainichi benkyou shimasu ka?* — Do you study every day?
21. **Time/place N1 から N2 まで。** — From N1 to/until N2.  
    *Ku-ji kara go-ji made hatarakimasu.* — I work from nine to five.
22. **N1 と N2** — N1 and N2 (complete list of nouns).  
    *Getsuyoubi to kayoubi.* — Monday and Tuesday.
23. **Sentence + ね。** — Seeks agreement or shared understanding.  
    *Ii tenki desu ne.* — Nice weather, isn't it?
24. **Time words without に** — Words such as *kyou, ashita, kinou, mainichi* usually take no に.  
    *Kyou benkyou shimasu.* — I will study today.

## Lesson 5 — Movement and transport

25. **Place へ 行きます／来ます／帰ります。** — Go/come/return to a place.  
    *Gakkou e ikimasu.* — I go to school.
26. **Place へ Transport で 行きます。** — Go to a place by transport.  
    *Densha de kaisha e ikimasu.* — I go to work by train.
27. **Person/animal と Vます。** — Do something with someone/animal.  
    *Tomodachi to ikimasu.* — I go with a friend.
28. **ひとりで Vます。** — Do something alone.  
    *Hitori de kaerimasu.* — I go home alone.
29. **いつ Vますか。** — When do/will you do it?  
    *Itsu Nihon e ikimasu ka?* — When will you go to Japan?
30. **Sentence + よ。** — Adds emphasis or new information.  
    *Ashita wa yasumi desu yo.* — Tomorrow is a day off, you know.

## Lesson 6 — Objects, actions, invitations

31. **N を Vます。** — **を** marks the direct object.  
    *Pan o tabemasu.* — I eat bread.
32. **N を します。** — Do/play an activity.  
    *Shukudai o shimasu.* — I do homework.
33. **何を しますか。** — What will/do you do?  
    *Nichiyoubi nani o shimasu ka?* — What will you do Sunday?
34. **Place で N を Vます。** — **で** marks the place where an action occurs.  
    *Toshokan de hon o yomimasu.* — I read books at the library.
35. **いっしょに Vませんか。** — Invitation: Would you like to…?  
    *Issho ni eiga o mimasen ka?* — Would you like to watch a movie together?
36. **Vます-stem + ましょう。** — Let’s do something.  
    *Ikimashou.* — Let’s go.

## Lesson 7 — Means, giving, receiving, teaching

37. **Tool/means/language で Vます。** — **で** marks a tool, method, or language.  
    *Hashi de tabemasu.* — I eat with chopsticks.
38. **N は 日本語で 何ですか。** — How do you say N in Japanese?  
    *“Book” wa Nihongo de nan desu ka?* — What is “book” in Japanese?
39. **Giver は Receiver に Thing を あげます。** — Give something to someone.  
    *Watashi wa Tom-san ni hon o agemasu.* — I give Tom a book.
40. **Receiver は Giver に／から Thing を もらいます。** — Receive something from someone.  
    *Watashi wa sensei kara hon o moraimashita.* — I received a book from the teacher.
41. **もう Vました。** — Already did.  
    *Mou tabemashita.* — I already ate.
42. **まだです。** — Not yet (common reply to a もう-question).  
    *Mou tabemashita ka? Iie, mada desu.* — Have you eaten? No, not yet.
43. **Borrower は Lender に／から N を 借ります。** — Borrow N from someone.  
    *Tomodachi kara hon o karimashita.* — I borrowed a book from a friend.
44. **Teacher は Student に Subject を 教えます。** — Teach someone a subject.  
    *Sensei wa watashi ni Nihongo o oshiemasu.* — The teacher teaches me Japanese.
45. **Student は Teacher に Subject を 習います。** — Learn a subject from someone.  
    *Watashi wa sensei ni Nihongo o naraimasu.* — I learn Japanese from the teacher.

## Lesson 8 — Adjectives

46. **N は い-adjective です。** — Describe a noun/subject with an い-adjective.  
    *Fuji-san wa takai desu.* — Mt. Fuji is tall.
47. **N は な-adjective です。** — Describe with a な-adjective (without な before です).  
    *Kono machi wa shizuka desu.* — This town is quiet.
48. **い-adjective: remove い + くないです。** — Negative い-adjective.  
    *Takaku nai desu.* — It is not expensive.
49. **な-adjective + じゃありません。** — Negative な-adjective.  
    *Genki ja arimasen.* — Not well/energetic.
50. **い-adjective + N.** — い-adjective directly modifies a noun.  
    *Oishii tabemono.* — Delicious food.
51. **な-adjective + な + N.** — な-adjective modifies a noun.  
    *Kirei na hana.* — A beautiful flower.
52. **とても + adjective。** — Very.  
    *Totemo atsui desu.* — It is very hot.
53. **あまり + negative adjective/sentence。** — Not very.  
    *Amari atsuku nai desu.* — It is not very hot.
54. **N は どうですか。** — How is N?  
    *Nihon no seikatsu wa dou desu ka?* — How is life in Japan?
55. **N1 は どんな N2 ですか。** — What kind of N2 is N1?  
    *Osaka wa donna machi desu ka?* — What kind of city is Osaka?
56. **Sentence 1 + が、Sentence 2。** — **が** means “but” and softens some requests.  
    *Oishii desu ga, takai desu.* — It is delicious, but expensive.

## Lesson 9 — Likes, ability, possession

57. **N が あります。** — Have/there is a non-living thing; **が** marks what exists/is possessed.  
    *Jikan ga arimasu.* — I have time.
58. **N が います。** — Have/there is a person or animal.  
    *Tomodachi ga imasu.* — I have a friend.
59. **N が 分かります。** — Understand N.  
    *Nihongo ga wakarimasu.* — I understand Japanese.
60. **N が 好きです。** — Like N.  
    *Ongaku ga suki desu.* — I like music.
61. **N が 嫌いです。** — Dislike N.  
    *Yasai ga kirai desu.* — I dislike vegetables.
62. **N が 上手です／下手です。** — Be good/bad at N.  
    *Ryouri ga jouzu desu.* — I am good at cooking.
63. **あまり + 好きじゃありません。** — Do not like very much (softer than 嫌い).  
    *Amari suki ja arimasen.* — I don’t like it very much.
64. **どうして + sentence か。** — Why?  
    *Doushite Nihongo ga suki desu ka?* — Why do you like Japanese?
65. **Reason から、result。** — Because/reason, result.  
    *Isogashii desu kara, ikimasen.* — Because I’m busy, I won’t go.

## Lesson 10 — Existence and positions

66. **N が います。** — A person/animal exists.  
    *Inu ga imasu.* — There is a dog.
67. **N が あります。** — A thing/place exists.  
    *Tsukue ga arimasu.* — There is a desk.
68. **Place に N が います／あります。** — Someone/something exists at a place; **に** marks the location of existence.  
    *Heya ni neko ga imasu.* — There is a cat in the room.
69. **N は Place に います／あります。** — Say where a known person/thing is; **は** marks the topic, **に** marks its location.  
    *Hon wa tsukue no ue ni arimasu.* — The book is on the desk.
70. **N1 の position word。** — Position relative to N1.  
    *Tsukue no shita.* — Under the desk.
71. **N1 や N2 など。** — N1, N2, and things like that; **や** gives examples, not a complete list.  
    *Hon ya zasshi nado.* — Books, magazines, and so on.
72. **N1 と N2。** — N1 and N2 as a complete list.  
    *Hon to zasshi.* — Books and magazines.
73. **どこ／だれ／何 + が います／ありますか。** — Ask what/who is present.  
    *Dare ga imasu ka?* — Who is there?
74. **N は どこに あります／いますか。** — Where is N?  
    *Terebi wa doko ni arimasu ka?* — Where is the TV?

## Lesson 11 — Counters, duration, frequency

75. **N を + quantity + V。** — Quantity usually comes before the verb.  
    *Ringo o itsutsu kaimashita.* — I bought five apples.
76. **People + が + counter + います。** — Number of people present/in a family.  
    *Kazoku ga yonin imasu.* — There are four people in my family.
77. **Time period + V。** — Duration for which an action occurs; usually no に for the duration itself.  
    *San-kagetsu Nihongo o benkyou shimashita.* — I studied Japanese for three months.
78. **Time period + に + number of times + V。** — Frequency per period; **に** means “per/in.”  
    *Isshuukan ni sankai undou shimasu.* — I exercise three times a week.
79. **N を + number + 枚／回 etc.** — Use the matching counter for flat things, times, etc.  
    *Kitte o ni-mai kudasai.* — Two stamps, please.
80. **N は 何人ですか／N を いくつ買いましたか。** — Ask how many people/things.  
    *Kazoku wa nan-nin desu ka?* — How many people are in your family?
81. **どのくらい + Vますか。** — How long/how much?  
    *Dono kurai benkyou shimashita ka?* — How long did you study?
82. **N だけ。** — Only N.  
    *Ringo o futatsu dake kaimashita.* — I bought only two apples.
83. **Place/time から Place/time まで + duration + かかります。** — It takes duration/time or money.  
    *Tokyo kara Osaka made san-jikan kakarimasu.* — It takes three hours from Tokyo to Osaka.
84. **Person は Place に います。** — Someone is/stays at a place; **に** marks where they are.  
    *Watashi wa gakkou ni imasu.* — I am at school.
85. **Number + ぐらい／くらい。** — Approximately/about.  
    *San-jikan gurai benkyou shimashita.* — I studied for about three hours.
86. **全部で + quantity/price。** — Total.  
    *Zenbu de ikura desu ka?* — How much is it in total?

## Lesson 12 — Past tense and comparisons

87. **N／な-adjective + でした。** — Was/were.  
    *Kinou wa ame deshita.* — It was rainy yesterday.
88. **N／な-adjective + じゃありませんでした。** — Was not/were not.  
    *Kinou wa hima ja arimasen deshita.* — I was not free yesterday.
89. **い-adjective: remove い + かったです。** — Past positive.  
    *Oishikatta desu.* — It was delicious.
90. **い-adjective: remove い + くなかったです。** — Past negative.  
    *Oishiku nakatta desu.* — It was not delicious.
91. **N は どうでしたか。** — How was N?  
    *Shiken wa dou deshita ka?* — How was the exam?
92. **N1 は N2 より adjective です。** — N1 is more adjective than N2; **より** marks the comparison standard.  
    *Nihon wa Indo yori chiisai desu.* — Japan is smaller than India.
93. **N1 のほうが N2 より adjective です。** — N1 is more adjective than N2 (explicitly identifies the preferred side).  
    *Indo no hou ga Nihon yori ookii desu.* — India is bigger than Japan.
94. **N1 と N2 と、どちらが adjective ですか。** — Which of the two is more adjective? **と** lists the two choices.  
    *Natsu to fuyu to, dochira ga atsui desu ka?* — Which is hotter, summer or winter?
95. **N のほうが adjective です。** — N is the more adjective one.  
    *Natsu no hou ga atsui desu.* — Summer is hotter.
96. **Group の中で + question word + が一番 + adjective ですか。** — Ask what/who is the most within a group; **で** marks the range/group.  
    *Doubutsu no naka de nani ga ichiban suki desu ka?* — Which animal do you like most?
97. **N が一番 + adjective です。** — N is the most adjective.  
    *Neko ga ichiban suki desu.* — I like cats the most.

## Lesson 13 — Wants, purpose, and ので

98. **N が ほしいです。** — I want a thing.  
    *Kuruma ga hoshii desu.* — I want a car.
99. **N が ほしくないです。** — I do not want a thing.  
    *Kuruma ga hoshikunai desu.* — I do not want a car.
100. **Vます-stem + たいです。** — Want to do V. Remove ます and add たい.  
     *Nihon e ikitai desu.* — I want to go to Japan.
101. **Vます-stem + たくないです。** — Do not want to do V.  
     *Tabetaku nai desu.* — I do not want to eat.
102. **Vます-stem + たかったです。** — Wanted to do V.  
     *Nihon e ikitakatta desu.* — I wanted to go to Japan.
103. **Vます-stem + たくなかったです。** — Did not want to do V.  
     *Ikitaku nakatta desu.* — I did not want to go.
104. **Person は Vます-stem + たがっています。** — Someone else appears to want to do V.  
     *Imouto wa kaimono shitagatte imasu.* — My younger sister wants to go shopping.
105. **Vませんか。** — Invite someone: Would you like to…?  
     *Issho ni tabemasen ka?* — Would you like to eat together?
106. **Vます-stem + ましょう。** — Let’s do V.  
     *Sanpo shimashou.* — Let’s take a walk.
107. **Place へ Vます-stem に 行きます／来ます／帰ります。** — Go/come/return to a place to do something; **に** marks purpose.  
     *Eiga o mi ni ikimasu.* — I’m going to watch a movie.
108. **Place へ 何をしに 行きますか。** — What are you going there to do?  
     *Toshokan e nani o shi ni ikimasu ka?* — What are you going to the library to do?
109. **Plain reason + ので、result。** — Because/since; a reason stated relatively softly.  
     *Ame na node, ikimasen.* — Since it’s raining, I won’t go.
110. **なにか + affirmative / 何も + negative。** — Something / nothing.  
     *Nani ka tabemasu.* — I’ll eat something. / *Nani mo tabemasen.* — I won’t eat anything.
111. **どこか。** — Somewhere.  
     *Dokoka e ikimasu.* — I’ll go somewhere.

## Lesson 14 — て-form, actions in progress, requests

112. **Vて、Vて、Vます。** — Connect actions in sequence.  
     *Asa jogingu o shite, shawaa o abite, kaisha e ikimasu.* — I jog, shower, then go to work.
113. **Group 1 て-form rules:** う／つ／る→って; む／ぶ／ぬ→んで; く→いて; ぐ→いで; す→して; 行く→行って.  
     *Kakimasu → kaite; yomimasu → yonde; ikimasu → itte.*
114. **Group 2: Vます → remove ます + て。**  
     *Tabemasu → tabete.* — eat → eating/て-form.
115. **Group 3: します→して; 来ます→来て。**  
     *Shimasu → shite; kimasu → kite.*
116. **Vて + います。** — Action in progress or continuing state.  
     *Ima benkyou shite imasu.* — I am studying now.
117. **Vて + いますか。** — Are you doing V?  
     *Nani o shite imasu ka?* — What are you doing?
118. **Vて + いません。** — Am/is/are not doing V.  
     *Benkyou shite imasen.* — I am not studying.
119. **Vて + いました。** — Was/were doing V.  
     *Benkyou shite imashita.* — I was studying.
120. **Vて + いませんでした。** — Was/were not doing V.  
     *Nani mo shite imasen deshita.* — I wasn’t doing anything.
121. **Vます-stem + ましょうか。** — Shall I/we…? Offer/help.  
     *Nimotsu o mochimashou ka?* — Shall I carry the luggage?
122. **Vて + ください。** — Please do V.  
     *Matte kudasai.* — Please wait.
123. **Sentence 1 + が、Sentence 2。** — **が** as “but,” or a polite softener before a request.  
     *Sumimasen ga, shio o totte kudasai.* — Excuse me, but please pass the salt.

## Lesson 15 — Permission, prohibition, state

124. **Vて + もいいですか。** — May I do V?  
     *Koko de shashin o totte mo ii desu ka?* — May I take pictures here?
125. **Vて + もいいです。** — It is okay to do V / permission is granted.  
     *ここで写真を撮ってもいいです。* — You may take pictures here.
126. **Vて + はいけません。** — Must not do V / prohibited.  
     *Koko de tabako o sutte wa ikemasen.* — You must not smoke here.
127. **Vて + います。** — A resulting state that continues.  
     *Kekkon shite imasu.* — I am married.
128. **Vて + います。** — Habitual/regular action.  
     *Mainichi Nihongo o benkyou shite imasu.* — I study Japanese every day.
129. **知っています／知りません。** — Know / do not know (use 知りません for “don’t know”).  
     *Miraa-san o shitte imasu ka? Iie, shirimasen.* — Do you know Mr. Miller? No, I don’t.

## Lesson 16 — Connecting descriptions and actions

130. **Vて、Vます。** — Connect sequential actions.  
     *Asa okite, gohan o tabete, gakkou e ikimasu.* — I get up, eat, and go to school.
131. **い-adjective: remove い + くて。** — Connect い-adjectives.  
     *Ookikute, atarashii desu.* — It is big and new.
132. **な-adjective／N + で。** — Connect な-adjectives and nouns.  
     *Kirei de, shizuka desu.* — It is beautiful and quiet.
133. **Vてから、V。** — After doing V1, do V2.  
     *Gohan o tabete kara, benkyou shimasu.* — I study after eating.
134. **N1 は N2 が adjective。** — N1 has/is characterized by N2.  
     *Maria-san wa kami ga nagai desu.* — Maria has long hair.
135. **どうやって + Vますか。** — How/by what method?  
     *Gakkou e dou yatte ikimasu ka?* — How do you get to school?
136. **どの + N。** — Which N?  
     *Dono basu desu ka?* — Which bus is it?

## Lesson 17 — ない-form, obligations, deadlines

137. **Group 1 ない-form:** change final う-row sound to あ-row + ない (う→わない).  
     *Kakimasu → kakanai; kaimasu → kawanai.*
138. **Group 2 ない-form:** remove ます + ない.  
     *Tabemasu → tabenai.*
139. **Group 3 ない-form:** します→しない; 来ます→来ない.  
     *Benkyou shimasu → benkyou shinai.*
140. **Vないでください。** — Please do not V.  
     *Wasurenaide kudasai.* — Please don’t forget.
141. **Vない-form + なければなりません。** — Must do V.  
     *薬を飲まなければなりません。* (*Kusuri o nomanakereba narimasen.*) — I must take medicine.
142. **Vない-form + なくてもいいです。** — Do not have to V.  
     *Ashita konakute mo ii desu.* — You don’t have to come tomorrow.
143. **N は + V。** — **は** marks the topic; in some contexts it contrasts the topic with something else.  
     *Kore wa watashi ga shimasu.* — I’ll do this one.
144. **Time + までに + V。** — By a deadline; **までに** means “by,” not “until.”  
     *Kinyoubi made ni repooto o dashimasu.* — I’ll submit the report by Friday.
145. **Time/place まで + V。** — Until/as far as; **まで** marks the endpoint.  
     *Go-ji made hatarakimasu.* — I work until five.
146. **Body part が 痛いです。** — A body part hurts.  
     *Atama ga itai desu.* — My head hurts.
147. **どうしましたか。** — What’s the matter?  
     *Dou shimashita ka?* — What’s wrong?

## Lesson 18 — Dictionary form and こと

148. **ます-form → dictionary form.** Group 1 changes final i-sound to u-sound; Group 2 adds る; Group 3 します→する, 来ます→来る.  
     *Tabemasu → taberu; kakimasu → kaku; shimasu → suru.*
149. **V dictionary + ことができます。** — Can/be able to do V.  
     *Kanji o yomu koto ga dekimasu.* — I can read kanji.
150. **N ができます。** — Can do/know an activity or skill.  
     *Sukii ga dekimasu.* — I can ski.
151. **V dictionary + こと。** — Turn an action into an activity/idea.  
     *Ongaku o kiku koto.* — Listening to music.
152. **趣味は V dictionary + ことです。** — My hobby is doing V.  
     *Watashi no shumi wa hon o yomu koto desu.* — My hobby is reading books.
153. **V dictionary + 前に、V。** — Before doing V, do another action.  
     *Neru mae ni hon o yomimasu.* — I read before sleeping.
154. **N の前に、V。** — Before N, do V.  
     *Shokuji no mae ni te o araいます.* — I wash my hands before meals.
155. **Time/quantity + 前に。** — Before a stated amount of time.  
     *San-nen mae ni Nihon e ikimashita.* — I went to Japan three years ago.
156. **なかなか + negative。** — Not easily/not as expected.  
     *Nakanaka dekimasen.* — I can’t do it easily.
157. **ぜひ + request/invitation/positive statement。** — By all means/definitely.  
     *Zehi kite kudasai.* — Please do come.

## Lesson 19 — Experiences, listing actions, becoming

158. **て-form → た-form (て→た; で→だ).** — Past plain verb form.  
     *Tabete → tabeta; nonde → nonda.*
159. **Vた + ことがあります。** — Have done V before.  
     *Nihon e itta koto ga arimasu.* — I have been to Japan before.
160. **Vた + ことがありません。** — Have never done V.  
     *Uma ni notta koto ga arimasen.* — I have never ridden a horse.
161. **Vたり、Vたりします。** — Do things such as V1 and V2.  
     *Nichiyoubi wa eiga o mitari, hon o yondari shimasu.* — On Sundays I do things like watch movies and read books.
162. **い-adjective: remove い + くなります。** — Become adjective.  
     *Atsuku narimasu.* — It becomes hot.
163. **な-adjective／N + に なります。** — Become adjective/noun.  
     *Genki ni narimashita.* — I became well.
164. **一度も + negative。** — Not even once/never.  
     *Ichido mo tabeta koto ga arimasen.* — I have never eaten it even once.

## Lesson 20 — Plain/casual forms

165. **Vます → plain non-past verb form.** ます-form becomes dictionary form.  
     *Ikimasu → iku; tabemasu → taberu.*
166. **Vません → Vない。** — Casual negative.  
     *Ikimasen → ikanai.*
167. **Vました → Vた。** — Casual past.  
     *Tabemashita → tabeta.*
168. **Vませんでした → Vなかった。** — Casual past negative.  
     *Iきませんでした → konakatta.*
169. **い-adjective + です → plain い-adjective.**  
     *Isogashii desu → isogashii.* — Busy.
170. **な-adjective + です → な-adjective + だ (affirmative plain form).**  
     *Genki desu → genki da.* — Well.
171. **Noun + です → Noun + だ (affirmative plain form).**  
     *Gakusei desu → gakusei da.* — Student.
172. **たいです → たい (plain form); たくないです → たくない.**  
     *Ikitai desu → ikitai.* — Want to go.
173. **Vたことがありません → Vたことがない。** — Casual “have never.”  
     *Itta koto ga nai.* — I have never been.
174. **Casual questions often omit か and use rising intonation.**  
     *Mou tabeta?* — Have you eaten already?
175. **Plain-form sentence + けど、sentence。** — But/though; casual connector.  
     *Ikitaikedo, jikan ga nai.* — I want to go, but I don’t have time.
176. **Vている → Vてる (casual contraction).**  
     *Motte iru → motteru.* — Have/possess.
177. **Casual は／を／が can sometimes be omitted when context is clear.** — Omission is conversational, not a universal rule.  
     *Kore (wa) taberu?* — Will you eat this?
178. **うん／ううん。** — Casual yes/no.  
     *Un, sou.* — Yeah, that’s right.

## Lesson 21 — Opinions, quotations, guesses

179. **Plain form + と 思います。** — I think that…; **と** marks the thought/content.  
     *Ashita ame ga furu to omoimasu.* — I think it will rain tomorrow.
180. **Negative plain form + と 思います。** — I think that something will not happen/is not true.  
     *Ame ga furanai to omoimasu.* — I think it won’t rain.
181. **N + について + どう思いますか。** — What do you think about N?  
     *Nihonについてどう思いますか?* (*Nihon ni tsuite dou omoimasu ka?*) — What do you think about Japan?
182. **そう思います。／そうは思いません。** — I think so / I don’t think so.  
     *Sou wa omoimasen.* — I don’t think so.
183. **Plain form + と 言います。** — Say that…; **と** marks the content being said.  
     *Tanaka-san wa iku to iimashita.* — Tanaka said he would go.
184. **「Exact quotation」+ と 言います。** — Say exact words.  
     *「ありがとう」と言いました。* (*“Arigatou” to iimashita.*) — I said “thank you.”
185. **Plain form + でしょう？** — Probably… / …right?  
     *Ashita wa samui deshou?* — It’ll be cold tomorrow, right?
186. **N + について。** — About/concerning N.  
     *Nihon ni tsuite hanashimasu.* — I’ll talk about Japan.

## Lesson 22 — Modifying nouns with descriptions

187. **Description + Noun.** — A word/phrase describing a noun comes before it.  
     *Atarashii uchi.* — A new house.
188. **[Plain-form sentence] + Noun.** — A clause describes the noun that follows.  
     *Kyoto e iku hito.* — The person who goes to Kyoto.
189. **V plain present/negative/past/past-negative + Noun.** — Different tenses describe the noun.  
     *Kyoto e ikanai hito.* — The person who doesn’t go to Kyoto.
190. **[Subject が + clause] + Noun.** — **が** marks the subject inside the modifying clause.  
     *Watashi ga kinou katta hon.* — The book I bought yesterday.
191. **Object/location/meeting particles can disappear when that noun becomes the modified noun.**  
     *Eiga o mita → watashi ga mita eiga.* — The movie I watched.  
     *Byouin de hataraite iru → hataraite iru byouin.* — The hospital where someone works.  
     *Tomodachi ni au → au tomodachi.* — The friend I will meet.
192. **V dictionary + 時間／約束／用事 + が あります。** — Have time/an appointment/something to do.  
     *Ashita tomodachi to au yakusoku ga arimasu.* — I have an appointment to meet a friend tomorrow.

## Lesson 23 — とき, と, movement paths

193. **V dictionary + とき。** — When/before doing V, depending on context.  
     *Nihon e iku toki, kamera o kaimashita.* — When I was going to Japan, I bought a camera.
194. **Vた + とき。** — When/after V is completed.  
     *Nihon e itta toki, kamera o kaimashita.* — When I went to Japan, I bought a camera.
195. **Vない + とき。** — When not doing V.  
     *Hima ja nai toki, benkyou shimasu.* — When I’m not free, I study. (Use the appropriate plain negative form for the verb/adjective.)
196. **い-adjective + とき。** — When it is adjective.  
     *Samui toki, koohii o nomimasu.* — When it’s cold, I drink coffee.
197. **な-adjective + な + とき。** — When it is a な-adjective.  
     *Hima na toki, eiga o mimasu.* — When I’m free, I watch movies.
198. **Noun + の + とき。** — When N.  
     *Kodomo no toki, yoku asobimashita.* — When I was a child, I often played.
199. **V dictionary + と、automatic/natural result。** — When/if V, a predictable result occurs.  
     *Kono botan o osu to, oto ga demasu.* — When you press this button, a sound comes out.
200. **N が + adjective/verb (inside a clause or scene description).** — **が** marks the subject being described or doing the action.  
     *Oto ga ookii desu.* — The sound is loud.
201. **Place を + movement verb.** — **を** marks a path/area traversed, not a direct object.  
     *Michi o arukimasu.* — I walk along the road.
202. **～と vs ～たら.** — **と** often gives an automatic result; **たら** can express a condition followed by intention/request/hope.  
     *Migi ni magaru to, eki ga arimasu.* — If you turn right, there is a station (expected result).

## Lesson 24 — Giving and receiving actions

203. **Someone は 私／my side に Thing を くれます。** — Someone gives something to me/my side.  
     *Satou-san wa watashi ni hana o kuremashita.* — Sato gave me flowers.
204. **A は B に Thing を あげます。** — A gives something to B (not normally used to describe someone giving to the speaker).  
     *Watashi wa imouto ni hon o agemashita.* — I gave my younger sister a book.
205. **Vて + あげます。** — Do an action for someone.  
     *Tomodachi ni Nihongo o oshiete agemashita.* — I taught Japanese to a friend.
206. **Vて + もらいます。** — Have/receive the favor of someone doing something.  
     *Sensei ni kanji o oshiete moraimashita.* — I had the teacher teach me kanji.
207. **Someone が／は Vて + くれます。** — Someone does something for me/my side.  
     *Tomodachi ga tetsudatte kuremashita.* — My friend helped me.
208. **N が V。** — **が** marks the subject/new information, including the person doing something for the speaker.  
     *Tanaka-san ga hon o kuremashita.* — Tanaka gave me a book.
209. **Question word + が Vますか。** — Ask who/what does an action or gives something.  
     *Dare ga tetsudatte kuremashita ka?* — Who helped you?
210. **Vます → て-form.** — Form required before あげる／もらう／くれる and other て-form patterns.  
     *Tetsudaimasu → tetsudatte.* — Help → help (て-form).

## Lesson 25 — If, even if, no matter how

211. **Vた + ら、result。** — If/when/after V happens.  
     *Uchi e kaettara, denwa shimasu.* — When I get home, I’ll call.
212. **い-adjective: remove い + かったら。** — If/when adjective condition is true.  
     *Yasukattara, kaいます.* — If it’s cheap, I’ll buy it.
213. **な-adjective／N + だったら。** — If N/な-adjective condition is true.  
     *Hima dattara, tetsudatte kudasai.* — If you’re free, please help.
214. **もし + ～たら。** — If/hypothetically; もし adds emphasis to the condition.  
     *Moshi jikan ga attara, eiga o mimasu.* — If I have time, I’ll watch a movie.
215. **Vて + も。** — Even if V.  
     *Ame ga futte mo, ikimasu.* — Even if it rains, I’ll go.
216. **い-adjective: remove い + くても。** — Even if adjective.  
     *Takakute mo, kaimasu.* — Even if it’s expensive, I’ll buy it.
217. **な-adjective + でも。** — Even if な-adjective.  
     *Hima demo, ikimasen.* — Even if I’m free, I won’t go.
218. **N + でも。** — Even if it is N.  
     *Nichiyoubi demo, hatarakimasu.* — Even on Sunday, I work.
219. **いくら + ～ても。** — No matter how/how much.  
     *Ikura takakute mo, kaimasu.* — No matter how expensive it is, I’ll buy it.
220. **N が inside a subordinate clause.** — **が** can mark the subject of the condition/clause.  
     *Tomodachi ga kitara, shuppatsu shimasu.* — When my friend arrives, we’ll leave.

# Particle index — each use kept separate

## は (wa)
- **N1 は N2 です** — topic: *Watashi wa gakusei desu.* — I am a student.
- **N は adjective/desu** — topic being described: *Kono machi wa shizuka desu.* — This town is quiet.
- **N は Place に います／あります** — known topic + its location: *Hon wa tsukue no ue ni arimasu.* — The book is on the desk.
- **N は N2 が adjective** — topic + feature: *Maria-san wa kami ga nagai desu.* — Maria has long hair.
- **N は N2 より adjective** — comparison topic: *Nihon wa Indo yori chiisai desu.* — Japan is smaller than India.
- **N は Vないでください／other instructions** — topic/contrast: *Kore wa watashi ga shimasu.* — I’ll do this one.

## が (ga)
- **N が あります** — thing that exists/is possessed: *Jikan ga arimasu.* — I have time.
- **N が います** — person/animal that exists: *Inu ga imasu.* — There is a dog.
- **N が 分かります** — thing understood: *Nihongo ga wakarimasu.* — I understand Japanese.
- **N が 好き／嫌い／上手／下手です** — thing liked/disliked or skill being evaluated: *Ongaku ga suki desu.* — I like music.
- **Place に N が います／あります** — newly introduced entity: *Heya ni neko ga imasu.* — There is a cat in the room.
- **N1 と N2 と、どちらが adjective?** — identifies the compared subject: *Dochira ga atsui desu ka?* — Which is hotter?
- **Group の中で N が一番 adjective** — the superlative subject: *Neko ga ichiban suki desu.* — I like cats the most.
- **N が痛いです** — body part that hurts: *Atama ga itai desu.* — My head hurts.
- **Plain clause + が + adjective/verb** — subject inside a modifying/subordinate clause: *Tomodachi ga kitara…* — When my friend comes…
- **Sentence + が、sentence** — contrast/softener (“but”): *Oishii desu ga, takai desu.* — It’s delicious, but expensive.

## を (o)
- **N を V** — direct object: *Pan o tabemasu.* — I eat bread.
- **Place を movement verb** — path traversed: *Michi o arukimasu.* — I walk along the road.
- **N を します** — activity/object of する: *Shukudai o shimasu.* — I do homework.
- **Vた sentence modifying N** — the original を can disappear when the object noun becomes the modified noun: *Eiga o mita → mita eiga.* — the movie watched.

## に (ni)
- **Specific time に V** — time point: *Roku-ji ni okimasu.* — I get up at six.
- **Place に N が いる／ある** — location of existence: *Heya ni neko ga imasu.* — A cat is in the room.
- **N は Place に いる／ある** — location of known entity: *Watashi wa gakkou ni imasu.* — I am at school.
- **Receiver に** — recipient of giving/teaching: *Tom-san ni hon o agemasu.* — I give Tom a book.
- **Giver に／から** — source with receive/borrow/learn verbs: *Sensei kara hon o moraいました.* — I received a book from the teacher.
- **Frequency period に count** — “per”: *Isshuukan ni sankai undou shimasu.* — I exercise three times per week.
- **Purpose に after ます-stem** — purpose of going/coming/returning: *Eiga o mi ni ikimasu.* — I go to watch a movie.
- **Deadline までに** — by a deadline: *Kinyoubi made ni dashimasu.* — I’ll submit it by Friday.
- **Vて-form + に?** — do not confuse with the **に** used in destination/recipient/purpose patterns; each verb may require a specific particle.

## で (de)
- **Place で action** — location where an action happens: *Toshokan de hon o yomimasu.* — I read at the library.
- **Tool/means/language で** — instrument/method: *Hashi de tabemasu.* — I eat with chopsticks.
- **Transport で** — means of transportation: *Densha de ikimasu.* — I go by train.
- **Group の中で / range で** — group/range of comparison: *Nihon de ichiban atsui…* — the hottest in Japan.
- **N で (connecting noun/な-adjective descriptions)** — “and”: *Kirei de shizuka desu.* — It is beautiful and quiet.
- **みんなで** — together/as a group: *Minna de tabemashou.* — Let’s eat together.

## と (to)
- **N1 と N2** — and (complete list): *Pan to ringo.* — Bread and apples.
- **Person と V** — with a person: *Tomodachi to ikimasu.* — I go with a friend.
- **N1 と N2 と、どちら…?** — lists two alternatives: *Natsu to fuyu to, dochira ga atsui?* — Which is hotter, summer or winter?
- **Plain form + と 思います** — marks thought/content: *Ame ga furu to omoimasu.* — I think it will rain.
- **Plain form + と 言います** — marks said content: *Iku to iimashita.* — Said that they would go.
- **V dictionary + と result** — natural/automatic result: *Botan o osu to, oto ga demasu.* — When you press the button, a sound comes out.

## の (no)
- **N1 の N2** — possession/association/origin/type: *Watashi no hon.* — My book.
- **N1 の position word** — location relative to an object: *Tsukue no shita.* — Under the desk.
- **N の前に** — before a noun/event: *Shokuji no mae ni.* — Before a meal.
- **N の中で** — within a group/range: *Doubutsu no naka de…* — Among animals…
- **N + の + とき** — when N: *Kodomo no toki.* — When I was a child.

## へ (e)
- **Place へ 行きます／来ます／帰ります** — direction/destination: *Gakkou e ikimasu.* — I go to school.
- **Place へ Vます-stem に 行きます** — destination plus purpose: *Eiga o mi ni ikimasu.* — I go to watch a movie.

## から／まで／までに
- **N1 から N2 まで** — from…to/until: *Ku-ji kara go-ji made.* — From nine to five.
- **Giver/source から** — from a person/organization: *Sensei kara moraいました.* — Received it from the teacher.
- **Place から** — starting point: *Tokyo kara Osaka made…* — From Tokyo to Osaka…
- **Time まで** — until: *Go-ji made hatarakimasu.* — I work until five.
- **Time までに** — by a deadline: *Kinyoubi made ni dashimasu.* — Submit by Friday.

## も／か／ね／よ／や／より／だけ／ので／ても
- **も** — also: *Watashi mo gakusei desu.* — I am also a student.
- **Vて + も** — even if: *Ame ga futte mo, ikimasu.* — Even if it rains, I’ll go.
- **か** — question marker: *Gakusei desu ka?* — Are you a student?
- **S1 か S2 か** — or: *Hon desu ka, zasshi desu ka?* — Is it a book or magazine?
- **ね** — seeks agreement: *Ii tenki desu ne.* — Nice weather, isn’t it?
- **よ** — emphasis/new information: *Ashita yasumi desu yo.* — Tomorrow is a day off, you know.
- **や** — non-exhaustive list: *Hon ya zasshi nado.* — Books, magazines, and so on.
- **より** — comparison standard: *Indo yori chiisai.* — Smaller than India.
- **だけ** — only: *Futatsu dake.* — Only two.
- **ので** — because/since: *Ame na node…* — Since it’s raining…
- **ても** — even if: *Takakute mo…* — Even if it’s expensive…

# Quick form-building reminders

- **て-form → た-form:** て→た; で→だ.
- **い-adjective past:** ～い→～かったです.
- **い-adjective past negative:** ～い→～くなかったです.
- **い-adjective negative:** ～い→～くないです.
- **い-adjective + くて:** connects descriptions.
- **な-adjective + で:** connects descriptions.
- **Vます-stem + たい:** want to do.
- **Vます-stem + に行く:** go to do.
- **V dictionary + ことができます:** can do.
- **V dictionary + 前に:** before doing.
- **Vた + ことがあります:** have done before.
- **Vた + ら:** if/when/after.
- **Vて + も:** even if.
- **い-adjective + くても:** even if it is…
- **な-adjective/N + でも:** even if it is…
