# SECTION 2: GRAMMAR

## 1. Noun Modification (～ + Noun)

**Meaning / Use:**  
Use a word or sentence to describe a noun.  
Japanese puts the description **before** the noun.

**Structure:**

```text
Description + Noun
```

**Explanation:**

- The describing part comes before the noun.
    
- A simple adjective can describe a noun.
    
- A whole sentence can also describe a noun.
    
- English usually has the noun before the description; Japanese does the opposite.
    

**Examples:**

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|新しい うち|atarashii uchi|a new house|
|きれいな うち|kirei na uchi|a beautiful house|
|京都へ行く人|Kyōto e iku hito|the person who goes to Kyoto|
|京都へ行った人|Kyōto e itta hito|the person who went to Kyoto|

**Common mistakes:**  
Do not put the description after the noun. Japanese uses **Description + Noun**.

---

## 2. Sentence + Noun (～ + N)

**Meaning / Use:**  
A whole sentence can describe a noun.  
The describing sentence comes directly before the noun.

**Structure:**

```text
[Describing sentence] + Noun
```

**Explanation:**

- The noun being described goes at the end.
    
- The sentence inside the description uses plain form.
    
- The noun can be an object, person, place, etc.
    
- Examples can express present, negative, past, past negative, and たい-form meanings.
    

**Examples:**

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|京都へ行く人|Kyōto e iku hito|the person who goes to Kyoto|
|京都へ行かない人|Kyōto e ikanai hito|the person who doesn't go to Kyoto|
|京都へ行った人|Kyōto e itta hito|the person who went to Kyoto|
|京都へ行きたい人|Kyōto e ikitai hito|the person who wants to go to Kyoto|

### Plain Form Required

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|行く人|iku hito|person who goes|
|行かない人|ikanai hito|person who doesn't go|
|行った人|itta hito|person who went|
|行かなかった人|ikanakatta hito|person who didn't go|

**Common mistakes:**  
❌ 京都へ行きます人  
✅ 京都へ行く人

## 3. A Sentence Can Modify a Noun — Particles を・で・に

**Meaning / Use:**  
When a noun is modified by a sentence, the noun can take the place of its original position.  
The particles **を, で, に** can disappear in this structure.

**Structure:**

```text
[Subject が + plain form] + Noun
```

**Explanation:**

- The noun being described moves to the end.
    
- **を** can disappear when the noun was originally the object.
    
- **で** can disappear when the noun was originally the location.
    
- **に** can disappear when the noun was originally the person being met.
    

**Examples:**

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|私が先週見た映画|watashi ga senshū mita eiga|the movie I watched last week|
|王さんが働いている病院|Wang-san ga hataraite iru byōin|the hospital where Mr. Wang works|
|私が明日会う友達|watashi ga ashita au tomodachi|the friend I will meet tomorrow|
|映画を見ました → 私が見た映画|eiga o mimashita → watashi ga mita eiga|I watched a movie → the movie I watched|

### Particle Changes

```text
映画を見た
→ 見た映画

病院で働いている
→ 働いている病院

友達に会う
→ 会う友達
```

**Common mistakes:**  
Do not keep **を・で・に** when the noun marked by that particle becomes the modified noun.

## 4. 「が」Inside the Modifying Sentence

**Meaning / Use:**  
When a sentence modifies a noun, the subject inside that modifying sentence is marked with **が**.

**Structure:**

```text
N + が + plain form + Noun
```

**Explanation:**

- The subject inside the modifying sentence uses **が**.
    
- **は** changes to **が** inside this modifying structure.
    
- The modified noun comes at the end.
    
- This creates a small sentence that describes the noun.
    

**Examples:**

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|ミラーさんが作ったケーキ|Mirā-san ga tsukutta kēki|the cake Mr. Miller made|
|カリナさんが書いた絵|Karina-san ga kaita e|the picture Ms. Karina drew|
|彼が生まれたところ|kare ga umareta tokoro|the place where he was born|
|あなたは彼が生まれたところを知っていますか。|anata wa kare ga umareta tokoro o shitte imasu ka|Do you know the place where he was born?|

**Common mistakes:**  
❌ ミラーさんは作ったケーキ  
✅ ミラーさんが作ったケーキ

## 5. Dictionary Form + 時間（じかん）

**Meaning / Use:**  
Use this to express **time to do something**.

**Structure:**

```text
V-dictionary + 時間
```

**Explanation:**

- Use the verb's dictionary form.
    
- 時間 means **time**.
    
- The pattern can be followed by **が あります / ありません**.
    
- It expresses having or not having time to do something.
    

**Examples:**

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|朝ご飯を食べる時間がありません。|asa gohan o taberu jikan ga arimasen|I have no time to eat breakfast.|
|勉強する時間がありません。|benkyō suru jikan ga arimasen|I don't have time to study.|
|寝る時間がありません。|neru jikan ga arimasen|I don't have time to sleep.|
|食べる時間がありません。|taberu jikan ga arimasen|I don't have time to eat.|

**Common mistakes:**  
Use the **dictionary form**, not the ます-form:  
❌ 食べます時間  
✅ 食べる時間

---

## 6. Dictionary Form + 約束（やくそく）

**Meaning / Use:**  
Use this to express an **arrangement / appointment to do something**.

**Structure:**

```text
V-dictionary + 約束が あります
```

**Explanation:**

- Use the dictionary form before 約束.
    
- 約束 means arrangement / appointment.
    
- **が あります** expresses that the arrangement exists.
    
- It can describe an arrangement with a friend.
    

**Examples:**

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|私は友達と映画を見る約束があります。|watashi wa tomodachi to eiga o miru yakusoku ga arimasu|I have an arrangement to see a movie with my friend.|
|明日会う約束があります。|ashita au yakusoku ga arimasu|I have an appointment to meet tomorrow.|
|友達と食べる約束があります。|tomodachi to taberu yakusoku ga arimasu|I have an arrangement to eat with my friend.|
|映画を見る約束|eiga o miru yakusoku|arrangement to watch a movie|

**Common mistakes:**  
Use **V-dictionary + 約束**, not the ます-form.

---

## 7. Dictionary Form + 用事（ようじ）

**Meaning / Use:**  
Use this to express **something to do / an errand**.

**Structure:**

```text
V-dictionary + 用事
```

**Explanation:**

- Use the dictionary form before 用事.
    
- 用事 means something to do / errand.
    
- **が あります** can be used to say that you have something to do.
    
- The pattern can describe the action connected to the errand.
    

**Examples:**

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|今日は市役所へ行く用事があります。|kyō wa shiyakusho e iku yōji ga arimasu|I have something to do at the city hall today.|
|行く用事|iku yōji|something to go/do|
|食べる用事|taberu yōji|something to eat|
|見る用事|miru yōji|something to watch|

**Common mistakes:**  
Use the **dictionary form** before 用事.

# 📝 Building Noun Modification

### Example: the movie I watched yesterday

**Step 1 — Original sentence**

**昨日映画を見ました。**  
**Kinō eiga o mimashita.**  
→ I watched a movie yesterday.

**Step 2 — Plain form**

**昨日見た**  
**kinō mita**  
→ watched yesterday

**Step 3 — Put the noun at the end**

**昨日見た映画**  
**kinō mita eiga**  
→ the movie I watched yesterday

### Formula

```text
Original sentence
        ↓
Make plain form
        ↓
Remove the noun from its original position
        ↓
Put the noun at the END
```
