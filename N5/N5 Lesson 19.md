# SECTION 2: Grammar

## 1. た-form (～た)

**Meaning / Use:**  
The た-form is a Japanese verb form used in several Lesson 19 patterns, especially past experiences and listing actions.

**Structure:**

> て → た  
> で → だ

**Explanation:**

- The た-form is closely related to the て-form.
- Make the て-form first, then change **て → た**.
- Make the で-form first, then change **で → だ**.
- It appears in **～たことがあります** and **～たり～たりします**.

**Examples:**

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|かいて → かいた|kaite → kaita|write → wrote|
|いって → いった|itte → itta|go → went|
|のんで → のんだ|nonde → nonda|drink → drank|
|たべて → たべた|tabete → tabeta|eat → ate|
|きて → きた|kite → kita|come → came|
|して → した|shite → shita|do → did|

**Common mistakes:**  
Do not change **て → た** and **で → だ** incorrectly.

---

## 2. ～たことがあります

**Meaning / Use:**  
Used to talk about past experiences.  
It means **"have done V before."**

**Structure:**

> Vた + こと + が + あります

**Explanation:**

- Use the verb in the **た-form**.
- **～たことがあります** talks about experience.
- It is different from simply using **V-mashita**.
- **V-mashita** describes a past event; **V-ta koto ga arimasu** describes experience.

**Examples:**

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|馬に乗ったことがあります。|Uma ni notta koto ga arimasu.|I have ridden a horse before.|
|北海道へ行ったことがあります。|Hokkaidou e itta koto ga arimasu.|I have been to Hokkaido before.|
|日本へ行ったことがあります。|Nihon e itta koto ga arimasu.|I have been to Japan before.|
|すしを食べたことがあります。|Sushi o tabeta koto ga arimasu.|I have eaten sushi before.|

**Common mistakes:**

- **V-mashita** = did something.
- **V-ta koto ga arimasu** = have done something before.

---

## 3. ～たことがありません

**Meaning / Use:**  
Used to say that you **have never done something**.

**Structure:**

> Vた + こと + が + ありません

**Explanation:**

- Use the **た-form** before こと.
- **～たことがありません** = have never done.
- **一度も** can be added for strong emphasis: "not even once."

**Examples:**

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|馬に乗ったことがありません。|Uma ni notta koto ga arimasen.|I have never ridden a horse.|
|相撲を見たことがありません。|Sumou o mita koto ga arimasen.|I have never watched sumo.|
|すしを食べたことがありません。|Sushi o tabeta koto ga arimasen.|I have never eaten sushi.|
|一度も馬に乗ったことがありません。|Ichido mo uma ni notta koto ga arimasen.|I have never ridden a horse even once.|

**Common mistakes:**  
**一度も** is used with the negative form.

---

## 4. ～たり、～たりします

**Meaning / Use:**  
Used to list examples of actions: **"do things like A and B."**

**Structure:**

> Vた + り、Vた + り + します

**Explanation:**

- Change each verb into the **た-form**.
- Add **り** after each た-form.
- The final action is followed by **します**.
- It gives examples of activities, not necessarily a complete list.

**Examples:**

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|テレビを見たり、映画を見たりします。|Terebi o mitari, eiga o mitari shimasu.|I do things like watch TV and movies.|
|ゴルフをしたり、相撲を見たりします。|Gorufu o shitari, sumou o mitari shimasu.|I do things like play golf and watch sumo.|
|本を読んだり、音楽を聞いたりします。|Hon o yondari, ongaku o kiitari shimasu.|I do things like read books and listen to music.|
|テレビを見たり、映画を見たりします。|Terebi o mitari, eiga o mitari shimasu.|I do things like watch TV and movies.|

**Common mistakes:**  
**～たり～たり** means examples of activities, not simply "and then."

---

## 5. ～て～て vs ～たり～たり

**Meaning / Use:**  
These patterns connect actions differently.

**Structure:**

> Vて、Vて + ました

**Explanation:**

- **～たり～たり** gives examples of activities.
- **～て～て** connects actions.
- **～て～て** can show a sequence of connected actions.
- Do not treat **～たり～たり** as simply "and then."

**Examples:**

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|テレビを見たり、映画を見たりします。|Terebi o mitari, eiga o mitari shimasu.|I do things like watch TV and movies.|
|テレビを見て、映画を見ました。|Terebi o mite, eiga o mimashita.|I watched TV and then watched a movie.|
|本を読んだり、音楽を聞いたりします。|Hon o yondari, ongaku o kiitari shimasu.|I do things like read books and listen to music.|
|ゴルフをしたり、相撲を見たりします。|Gorufu o shitari, sumou o mitari shimasu.|I do things like play golf and watch sumo.|

**Common mistakes:**

- **～たり～たり** → examples / things like
- **～て～て** → connected actions / sequence

---

## 6. ～くなります

**Meaning / Use:**  
Used with **い-adjectives** to mean **"become..."**

**Structure:**

> い-adjective → remove い + くなります

**Explanation:**

- Start with an い-adjective.
- Remove the final **い**.
- Add **くなります**.
- It expresses a change of state.

**Examples:**

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|寒くなります。|Samuku narimasu.|It becomes cold.|
|暑くなります。|Atsuku narimasu.|It becomes hot.|
|安くなります。|Yasuku narimasu.|It becomes cheap.|
|眠くなります。|Nemuku narimasu.|I become sleepy.|
|寒くなりました。|Samuku narimashita.|It became cold.|

**Common mistakes:**  
Do not keep the final **い**:  
**samui → samuku**, not _samui-ku_.

---

## 7. ～になります

**Meaning / Use:**  
Used with **な-adjectives and nouns** to mean **"become..."**

**Structure:**

> な-adjective + に + なります  
> Noun + に + なります

**Explanation:**

- な-adjective + **に なります**
- Noun + **に なります**
- The な is not used before **に なります**.
- It expresses a change of state or status.

**Examples:**

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|元気になります。|Genki ni narimasu.|Become healthy / get well.|
|元気になりました。|Genki ni narimashita.|I got well / became healthy.|
|25歳になります。|Nijuu-go-sai ni narimasu.|Become 25 years old.|
|25歳になりました。|Nijuu-go-sai ni narimashita.|I became 25 years old.|

**Common mistakes:**  
With a な-adjective, use **に**, not **な**:

> 元気な → 元気に なります

---

## 8. ～くなる vs ～になる

**Meaning / Use:**  
Both express **"become..."**, but the form depends on the word before it.

**Structure:**

> い-adjective → Aく + なります  
> な-adjective → Aに + なります  
> Noun → Nに + なります

**Explanation:**

- **い-adjective:** remove い and add **く**.
- **な-adjective:** add **に**.
- **Noun:** add **に**.
- The correct form depends on the type of word.

**Examples:**

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|寒い → 寒くなります。|Samui → samuku narimasu.|become cold|
|眠い → 眠くなります。|Nemui → nemuku narimasu.|become sleepy|
|元気な → 元気になります。|Genki na → genki ni narimasu.|become healthy|
|25歳 → 25歳になります。|Nijuu-go-sai → nijuu-go-sai ni narimasu.|become 25 years old|

---

## 9. そうですね

**Meaning / Use:**  
Used to agree or sympathize with what someone says.

**Structure:**

> そう + です + ね

**Explanation:**

- **そうですね** can mean "That's right."
- It can mean "Yes, it is."
- It can express agreement or sympathy.
- It is different from **そうですか**.

**Examples:**

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|寒くなりましたね。そうですね。|Samuku narimashita ne. Sou desu ne.|It has become cold, hasn't it? Yes, it has.|
|乾杯！|Kanpai!|Cheers!|
|実は……|Jitsu wa...|Actually... / To tell the truth...|
|そうですね。|Sou desu ne.|Yes, that's right / I agree.|

---

## 10. そうですね vs そうですか

**Meaning / Use:**  
These expressions have different uses in conversation.

**Structure:**

> そうです + ね  
> そうです + か

**Explanation:**

- **そうですね** → agreement / sympathy.
- **そうですか** → surprise / confirmation / "I see."
- **ね** is used for agreement or shared understanding.
- **か** is used for a question or reaction.

**Examples:**

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|寒くなりましたね。|Samuku narimashita ne.|It's gotten cold, hasn't it?|
|そうですね。|Sou desu ne.|Yes, it has.|
|明日帰ります。|Ashita kaerimasu.|I'm going home tomorrow.|
|そうですか。|Sou desu ka.|Oh, I see.|
