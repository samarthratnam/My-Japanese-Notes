# 🇯🇵 Japanese Particles — N5 Cheat Sheet

> [!tip] Quick idea
> Japanese particles tell you the **role of a word in a sentence**.
>
> Example:
> **わたしは バスで がっこうに いきます。**
>
> - わたし**は** → topic
> - バス**で** → transportation
> - がっこう**に** → destination
>
> → I go to school by bus.

---

# 1. は — *wa* → Topic

> **N は ...** = As for N...

Used to tell us **what the sentence is about**.

### Example

> わたしは がくせいです。  
> *Watashi wa gakusei desu.*  
> → I am a student.

### 🧠 Think

**は = "Speaking of..."**

> [!warning] Special pronunciation
> Written **は**, but pronounced **wa** when it is a particle.

---

# 2. が — *ga* → Subject / Thing Being Identified

Used for the **subject**, especially with certain expressions.

## A. Who / What Exists

> いぬが います。  
> *Inu ga imasu.*  
> → There is a dog.

> テレビが あります。  
> *Terebi ga arimasu.*  
> → There is a TV.

## B. Important Patterns

| Pattern | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **N が すきです** | Like N |
| **N が きらいです** | Dislike N |
| **N が じょうずです** | Good at N |
| **N が へたです** | Poor at N |
| **N が わかります** | Understand N |

### Examples

> おんがくが すきです。  
> *Ongaku ga suki desu.*  
> → I like music.

> にほんごが わかります。  
> *Nihongo ga wakarimasu.*  
> → I understand Japanese.

### 🧠 Biggest rule

> **すき・きらい・じょうず・へた・わかります → が**

---

# 3. を — *o* → Object

Marks **what you do the action to**.

> ごはんを たべます。  
> *Gohan o tabemasu.*  
> → I eat rice.

> ほんを よみます。  
> *Hon o yomimasu.*  
> → I read a book.

## ⚠️ Special Case: おります

**おります → を**

> でんしゃを おります。  
> *Densha o orimasu.*  
> → I get off the train.

### 🧠 Important pair

| Verb | Particle | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **のります** | **に** | Get on |
| **おります** | **を** | Get off |

> **でんしゃに のります。**  
> Get on the train.
>
> **でんしゃを おります。**  
> Get off the train.

---

# 4. に — *ni* → Time / Destination / Location / Target

> [!important]
> **に** is one of the most important N5 particles.

## A. Specific Time

> 7じに おきます。  
> *Shichi-ji ni okimasu.*  
> → I wake up **at 7**.

> 10じに ねます。  
> *Juu-ji ni nemasu.*  
> → I sleep **at 10**.

### Pattern

**Specific time + に**

---

## B. Destination

> がっこうに いきます。  
> *Gakkou ni ikimasu.*  
> → I go **to school**.

> くうこうに いきます。  
> *Kuukou ni ikimasu.*  
> → I go **to the airport**.

### Pattern

**Destination + に + いきます**

---

## C. Location of Existence

> へやに ねこが います。  
> *Heya ni neko ga imasu.*  
> → There is a cat **in the room**.

> つくえの うえに ほんが あります。  
> *Tsukue no ue ni hon ga arimasu.*  
> → There is a book **on the desk**.

### Pattern

**Place + に + N + あります / います**

---

## D. Getting Onto Something

> でんしゃに のります。  
> *Densha ni norimasu.*  
> → Get on the train.

### 🧠 Think

**に = AT / TO / IN / ONTO**

---

# 5. で — *de* → Place of Action / Means

## A. Place Where an Action Happens

> がっこうで べんきょうします。  
> *Gakkou de benkyou shimasu.*  
> → I study **at school**.

> こうえんで あるきます。  
> *Kouen de arukimasu.*  
> → I walk **in the park**.

### Pattern

**Place + で + Action**

---

## B. Means of Transportation

> バスで いきます。  
> *Basu de ikimasu.*  
> → I go **by bus**.

> でんしゃで いきます。  
> *Densha de ikimasu.*  
> → I go **by train**.

### Pattern

**Transportation + で + いきます**

---

## 🧠 Important: に vs で

### Destination → に

> がっこう**に** いきます。  
> → I go **to** school.

### Action location → で

> がっこう**で** べんきょうします。  
> → I study **at** school.

### Transportation → で

> バス**で** いきます。  
> → I go **by** bus.

> [!tip] Easy memory
> **に = Where you're going**
>
> **で = Where you do something / How you travel**

---

# 6. の — *no* → Possession / Relationship

Connects two nouns.

> わたしの ほん  
> *Watashi no hon*  
> → My book

### Basic pattern

**A の B = B related to A**

---

## Examples

> つくえの うえ  
> *Tsukue no ue*  
> → On top of the desk.

> へやの なか  
> *Heya no naka*  
> → Inside the room.

> ぎんこうの となり  
> *Ginkou no tonari*  
> → Next to the bank.

### 🧠 Think

**の = A's / belonging to / related to**

---

# 7. と — *to* → And / With

## A. And

> りんごと バナナ  
> *Ringo to banana*  
> → Apples **and** bananas.

### Pattern

**A と B = A and B**

---

## B. With

> ともだちと いきます。  
> *Tomodachi to ikimasu.*  
> → I go **with my friend**.

### Pattern

**Person + と + Action**

### 🧠 Think

**と = AND / WITH**

---

# 8. も — *mo* → Also / Too

> わたしも がくせいです。  
> *Watashi mo gakusei desu.*  
> → I am **also** a student.

Instead of:

> わたしは

You can say:

> わたしも  
> → I **too / also**

### 🧠 Think

**も = also / too**

---

# 9. から — *kara* → From

Used for the **starting point**.

## A. Time

> 9じから  
> *Ku-ji kara*  
> → From 9 o'clock.

## B. With まで

> 9じから 5じまで  
> *Ku-ji kara go-ji made*  
> → From 9 to 5.

### 🧠 Think

**から = FROM**

---

# 10. まで — *made* → Until / Up To

Marks the **ending point**.

> 5じまで  
> *Go-ji made*  
> → Until 5.

> 9じから 5じまで  
> *Ku-ji kara go-ji made*  
> → From 9 to 5.

### 🧠 Important pair

**から → FROM**

**まで → UNTIL**

---

# 11. へ — *e* → Toward / To

Used for **direction / destination**.

> にほんへ いきます。  
> *Nihon e ikimasu.*  
> → I go **to Japan**.

### に vs へ

For N5:

**に → destination**

**へ → direction / toward**

Both can work:

> がっこうに いきます。  
> *Gakkou ni ikimasu.*

> がっこうへ いきます。  
> *Gakkou e ikimasu.*

Both mean:

→ I go to school.

> [!warning] Special pronunciation
> Written **へ**, but pronounced **e** when it is a particle.

---

# 12. か — *ka* → Question

Put it at the end of a sentence.

> がくせいですか。  
> *Gakusei desu ka?*  
> → Are you a student?

> どこですか。  
> *Doko desu ka?*  
> → Where is it?

### 🧠 Think

**か = ?**

---

# 13. ね — *ne* → "Right?" / Agreement

Used when seeking agreement or confirmation.

> いい てんきですね。  
> *Ii tenki desu ne.*  
> → Nice weather, isn't it?

### 🧠 Think

**ね = right? / isn't it?**

---

# 14. よ — *yo* → Emphasis / New Information

Used when **telling the listener something** or emphasizing information.

> これは おいしいですよ。  
> *Kore wa oishii desu yo.*  
> → This is delicious, you know!

### 🧠 Think

**よ = I'm telling you / emphasis**

---

# 15. や — *ya* → Examples / "And Things Like That"

Useful for giving **examples from a larger list**.

> パンや りんごを かいました。  
> *Pan ya ringo o kaimashita.*  
> → I bought bread, apples, **and things like that**.

## と vs や

### と = Complete List

> パンと りんご  
> → Bread **and** apples.

Implies these are the items being listed.

### や = Examples

> パンや りんご  
> → Bread, apples, **etc.**

There may be other things too.

### 🧠 Think

**と = complete list**

**や = examples + etc.**

---

# 🧠 THE MASTER TABLE

| Particle | Romaji | Main Use | Think |
|---|---|---|---|
| **は** | wa | Topic | **As for...** |
| **が** | ga | Subject / existence / certain patterns | **Who / what?** |
| **を** | o | Object | **What is acted on?** |
| **に** | ni | Time / destination / existence / target | **at / to / in / onto** |
| **で** | de | Action place / transportation | **where action / by** |
| **の** | no | Possession / relationship | **A's B** |
| **と** | to | And / with | **and / with** |
| **も** | mo | Also / too | **also** |
| **から** | kara | Starting point | **from** |
| **まで** | made | Ending point | **until** |
| **へ** | e | Direction | **toward / to** |
| **か** | ka | Question | **?** |
| **ね** | ne | Agreement | **right?** |
| **よ** | yo | Emphasis / new information | **you know!** |
| **や** | ya | Examples | **A, B, etc.** |

# 🔥 MOST IMPORTANT 8 PATTERNS

> [!important] Focus on these first
> These are the particle patterns you should be able to recognize immediately.

## ① Specific Time → に

> 7じ**に** おきます。  
> → Wake up **at 7**.

---

## ② Destination → に / へ

> がっこう**に** いきます。  
> → Go **to school**.

> がっこう**へ** いきます。  
> → Go **toward/to school**.

---

## ③ Action Location → で

> がっこう**で** べんきょうします。  
> → Study **at school**.

---

## ④ Transportation → で

> バス**で** いきます。  
> → Go **by bus**.

---

## ⑤ Get ON → に

> バス**に** のります。  
> → Get **on the bus**.

---

## ⑥ Get OFF → を

> バス**を** おります。  
> → Get **off the bus**.

---

## ⑦ Like / Dislike / Good / Bad / Understand → が

> おんがく**が** すきです。  
> → I like music.

> にほんご**が** わかります。  
> → I understand Japanese.

---

## ⑧ Existence / Location → に

> へや**に** ねこが います。  
> → There is a cat **in the room**.

> つくえの うえ**に** ほんが あります。  
> → There is a book **on the desk**.
