# Lesson 3 – Grammar

---

# I. Location of Things

### Structure

**N1 wa N2 desu.**

When **N2** is a place.

Meaning:

> **N1 is in/on/at N2.**

Examples

```
Hon wa tsukue no ue desu.
The book is on the desk.

Terebi wa heya desu.
The television is in the room.

Jimusho wa ni-kai desu.
The office is on the second floor.

Watashi wa kyōshitsu desu.
I am in the classroom.
```

---

# II. Asking Location

### Structure

**N wa doko desu ka?**

or (Polite)

**N wa dochira desu ka?**

Meaning

> Where is N?

Examples

```
Toire wa doko desu ka?
Where is the toilet?

Jimusho wa doko desu ka?
Where is the office?

Uketsuke wa dochira desu ka?
Where is the reception?

Kaigishitsu wa dochira desu ka?
Where is the conference room?
```

---

### Asking Origin

**Kuni wa dochira desu ka?**

Meaning

> Which country are you from?

Example

```
Kuni wa dochira desu ka?

Indo desu.
I am from India.
```

---

### Asking Which Floor

```
Toire wa nan-gai desu ka?
Which floor is the toilet on?

San-gai desu.
It is on the third floor.
```

---

# III. N1 no N2

### Structure

**N1 no N2**

Meaning

- N2 belongs to N1.
- N2 is from N1.
- N2 is made in N1 (country).

Examples

```
Nihon no kuruma
Japanese car

Indo no tabemono
Indian food

Amerika no eiga
American movie

Nihon no kaisha
Japanese company

Sensei no kaban
Teacher's bag

Watashi no hon
My book
```

---

# IV. Honorific Prefix 「お (O-)」

Adding **お (o-)** before some nouns makes them more polite and respectful.

Examples

|Normal|Polite|
|---|---|
|Kuni|Okuni|
|Sake|Osake|
|Hanami|Ohanami|
|Namae|Onamae|
|Kane|Okane|
|Cha|Ocha|

> **Correction:** "O" cannot be added to every word. It is only used with certain common nouns.
