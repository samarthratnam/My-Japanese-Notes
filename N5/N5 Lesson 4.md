# Lesson 4 – Grammar

---

# I. Time + Verb

### Structure

**Time + に (ni) + Verb**

**N (Time) + ni + Verb**

Meaning

> Do an action at a specific time.

Examples

```
Watashi wa roku-ji ni okimasu.
I wake up at 6 o'clock.

Mainichi shichi-ji ni nemasu.
I sleep at 7 every day.

Hachi-ji ni benkyō shimasu.
I study at 8 o'clock.

Kesa roku-ji ni okimashita.
I woke up at 6 this morning.
```

> **Important:** Use **に (ni)** only with **specific times** (7:00, Monday, 3 PM). Do **not** use **ni** with words like **kyō**, **ashita**, **kinō**, **mainichi**, etc.

Correct:

- Kyō benkyō shimasu.
- Mainichi hatarakimasu.

---

# II. Negative Verb

### Structure

**Verb (ます form) → Verbません**

Meaning

> Do not do.

Examples

```
Nemasu → Nemasen
Sleep → Do not sleep

Ikimasu → Ikimasen
Go → Do not go

Benkyō shimasu → Benkyō shimasen
Do not study
```

Sentence Examples

```
Watashi wa kyō benkyō shimasen.
I will not study today.

Ashita hatarakimasen.
I will not work tomorrow.

Nichiyōbi ni ginkō e ikimasen.
I do not go to the bank on Sunday.
```

---

# III. Question

### Structure

**Verbますか？**

Meaning

> Do you...?

Examples

```
Nemasu ka?
Do you sleep?

Benkyō shimasu ka?
Do you study?

Mainichi hatarakimasu ka?
Do you work every day?
```

Answers

```
Hai, shimasu.
Yes, I do.

Iie, shimasen.
No, I don't.
```

---

# IV. From...To...

### Structure

**N1 kara N2 made**

Meaning

> From N1 until N2.

Examples

```
Ku-ji kara go-ji made hatarakimasu.
I work from 9 to 5.

Getsuyōbi kara Kinyōbi made benkyō shimasu.
I study from Monday to Friday.

Asa roku-ji kara hachi-ji made undō shimasu.
I exercise from 6 AM to 8 AM.
```

---

# V. From...To... (Place)

### Structure

**Place1 kara Place2 made**

Examples

```
Uchi kara daigaku made ikimasu.
I go from home to the university.

Ginkō kara yūbinkyoku made arukimasu.
I walk from the bank to the post office.
```

---

# VI. Connecting Nouns

### Structure

**N1 to N2**

Meaning

> N1 and N2.

Examples

```
Pan to kōhī
Bread and coffee

Hon to jisho
Book and dictionary

Watashi to tomodachi
Me and my friend
```

---

# VII. Sentence Ending 「ね」

**ね (ne)** is used to:

- seek agreement
- sound friendly
- confirm information

Examples

```
Kono hon wa omoshiroi desu ne.
This book is interesting, isn't it?

Kyō wa atsui desu ne.
It's hot today, isn't it?

Takai desu ne.
It's expensive, isn't it?
```
