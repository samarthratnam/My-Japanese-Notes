# SECTION 2: 📖 GRAMMAR

## 1. ～て、～て — Connecting Actions

**Meaning / Use:**  
Connect two or more actions in the order they happen.

**Structure:**

> **V-te + V-te + V-masu**

**Explanation:**

- Shows actions happening in sequence.
    
- Think: **A → B → C**
    
- Each action before the last one uses the **て-form**.
    
- The final verb can use **～ます**.
    

**Examples:**

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|朝、ジョギングをして、シャワーを浴びて、会社へ行きます。|Asa, jogingu o shite, shawaa o abite, kaisha e ikimasu.|In the morning, I jog, take a shower, and go to work.|
|京都駅から16番のバスに乗って、大学前で降ります。|Kyoto-eki kara juuroku-ban no basu ni notte, Daigaku-mae de orimasu.|Take bus number 16 from Kyoto Station and get off at Daigaku-mae.|
|朝、ジョギングをして、シャワーを浴びます。|Asa, jogingu o shite, shawaa o abimasu.|In the morning, I jog and take a shower.|
|バスに乗って、大学前で降ります。|Basu ni notte, Daigaku-mae de orimasu.|Get on the bus and get off at Daigaku-mae.|

**Common mistakes:**  
Do not use **～て** as the final action when the sentence is meant to end with the main action.

---

## 2. い-Adjective → ～くて

**Meaning / Use:**  
Connect two **い-adjectives**.

**Structure:**

> **い-adjective: Remove い + くて**

**Explanation:**

- Change the final **い → くて**.
    
- Formula: **i → kute**
    
- Used when connecting related descriptions.
    
- **いい → よくて** is irregular.
    

**Examples:**

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|この家は大きくて、新しいです。|Kono ie wa ookikute, atarashii desu.|This house is big and new.|
|大きい → 大きくて|Ookii → ookikute|Big → big and...|
|小さい → 小さくて|Chiisai → chiisakute|Small → small and...|
|いい → よくて|Ii → yokute|Good → good and...|

**Common mistakes:**  
**いい → いくて** is incorrect. Use **よくて**.

---

## 3. N / な-Adjective + で

**Meaning / Use:**  
Connect a **noun** or **な-adjective** with another description.

**Structure:**

> **Noun + です → Noun + で**  
> **な-adjective + です → な-adjective + で**

**Explanation:**

- For nouns: **です → で**.
    
- For な-adjectives: **です → で**.
    
- This connects related ideas.
    
- For い-adjectives, use **～くて** instead.
    

**Examples:**

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|この町はきれいで、静かです。|Kono machi wa kirei de, shizuka desu.|This town is beautiful and quiet.|
|学生です → 学生で|Gakusei desu → gakusei de|Student → being a student and...|
|カリナさんはインドネシア人で、京都大学の留学生です。|Karina-san wa Indoneshiajin de, Kyoto daigaku no ryuugakusei desu.|Karina is Indonesian and a student at Kyoto University.|
|きれいです → きれいで|Kirei desu → kirei de|Beautiful → beautiful and...|

### ⭐ Easy Rule

|Type|Change|
|---|---|
|い-adjective|**i → kute**|
|な-adjective|**desu → de**|
|Noun|**desu → de**|

### ⚠️ Contradictory Ideas

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|この部屋は狭いです。でも、きれいです。|Kono heya wa semai desu. Demo, kirei desu.|This room is small. But it is clean.|

**Common mistakes:**  
Use **でも (demo)** when the ideas contrast rather than connect naturally.

## 4. Vてから、V

**Meaning / Use:**  
After doing **A**, do **B**.

**Structure:**

> **V-te kara, V**

**Explanation:**

- The first action is completed before the second action.
    
- **～てから** means **after doing...**
    
- The order is clear: **A → B**.
    
- Compare **Vて、V** with **Vてから、V**.
    

**Examples:**

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|国へ帰ってから、父の会社で働きます。|Kuni e kaette kara, chichi no kaisha de hatarakimasu.|After I return to my country, I will work at my father's company.|
|コンサートが終わってから、レストランへ行きました。|Konsaato ga owatte kara, resutoran e ikimashita.|After the concert ended, I went to a restaurant.|
|国へ帰ってから、働きます。|Kuni e kaette kara, hatarakimasu.|After returning to my country, I will work.|
|コンサートが終わってから、レストランへ行きました。|Konsaato ga owatte kara, resutoran e ikimashita.|After the concert ended, I went to a restaurant.|

### ～て vs ～てから

|Pattern|Meaning|
|---|---|
|**V-te, V**|A, then B|
|**V-te kara, V**|After finishing A, do B|

**Common mistakes:**  
**～てから** specifically shows that the first action is completed before the second.

## 5. Nは Nが + Adjective

**Meaning / Use:**  
Describe a **part or feature** of a person or thing.

**Structure:**

> **N1 は N2 が Adjective**

**Explanation:**

- **N1** = person or thing being described.
    
- **N2** = part or feature.
    
- **は** marks the main topic.
    
- **が** marks the part or feature.
    

**Examples:**

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|大阪は食べ物がおいしいです。|Oosaka wa tabemono ga oishii desu.|Food in Osaka is delicious.|
|マリアさんは髪が長いです。|Maria-san wa kami ga nagai desu.|Maria has long hair.|
|髪が長いです。|Kami ga nagai desu.|Hair is long.|
|背が高いです。|Se ga takai desu.|The person is tall.|

### Useful Patterns

|Pattern|Meaning|
|---|---|
|kami ga nagai|hair is long|
|me ga ookii|eyes are big|
|se ga takai|person is tall|

---

## 6. どうやって

**Meaning / Use:**  
Ask **how** or **in what way** something is done.

**Structure:**

> **どうやって + Sentence?**

**Explanation:**

- Used to ask about the **method / way**.
    
- **どうやって** = how?
    
- It asks how someone does something.
    
- The answer can describe the actions used.
    

**Examples:**

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|大学へどうやって行きますか。|Daigaku e dou yatte ikimasu ka?|How do you go to university?|
|京都駅から16番のバスに乗って、大学前で降ります。|Kyoto-eki kara juuroku-ban no basu ni notte, Daigaku-mae de orimasu.|Take bus number 16 from Kyoto Station and get off at Daigaku-mae.|
|どうやって行きますか。|Dou yatte ikimasu ka?|How do you go?|
|どうやって大学へ行きますか。|Dou yatte daigaku e ikimasu ka?|How do you go to university?|

**Common mistakes:**  
**どうやって** asks about the **way / method**, not simply the destination.

---

## 7. どの + N

**Meaning / Use:**  
Ask **which + noun?**

**Structure:**

> **どの + Noun**

**Explanation:**

- **どの** must be followed by a noun.
    
- Used when choosing or identifying **which person or thing**.
    
- Pattern: **どの + noun**
    
- Example: **どの人** = which person?
    

**Examples:**

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|どの人が髪が黒いですか。|Dono hito ga kami ga kuroi desu ka?|Which person has black hair?|
|あの人が髪が黒いです。|Ano hito ga kami ga kuroi desu.|That person has black hair.|
|どの人ですか。|Dono hito desu ka?|Which person is it?|
|どの人が髪が黒いですか。|Dono hito ga kami ga kuroi desu ka?|Which person has black hair?|

**Common mistakes:**  
Do not use **どの** alone. Use **どの + noun**.
