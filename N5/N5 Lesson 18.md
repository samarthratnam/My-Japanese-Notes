# SECTION 2: 📖 Grammar

## 1. Dictionary Form (辞書形)

**Meaning / Use:** The dictionary form is the basic form of a verb. It is normally found in a Japanese dictionary.

**Structure:**

`ます-form → Dictionary form`

**Explanation:**

- **Group I:** Change the final **i-sound** of the ます-form to the corresponding **u-sound**.
- **Group II:** Add **る** to the verb stem.
- **Group III:** します → する and きます → 来る.
- Dictionary form is used in several Lesson 18 patterns.

### Group I

|ます-form|Dictionary form|Romaji|
|---|---|---|
|かきます|書く|kaku|
|よみます|読む|yomu|
|とります|取る|toru|
|すいます|吸う|suu|
|はなします|話す|hanasu|
|いそぎます|急ぐ|isogu|
|あそびます|遊ぶ|asobu|
|まちます|待つ|matsu|
|しにます|死ぬ|shinu|

### Group II

|ます-form|Dictionary form|Romaji|
|---|---|---|
|たべます|食べる|taberu|
|みます|見る|miru|
|おきます|起きる|okiru|

### Group III

|ます-form|Dictionary form|Romaji|
|---|---|---|
|します|する|suru|
|きます|来る|kuru|

**Examples:**

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|かきます → 書く|kakimasu → kaku|write → to write|
|よみます → 読む|yomimasu → yomu|read → to read|
|たべます → 食べる|tabemasu → taberu|eat → to eat|
|します → する|shimasu → suru|do → to do|

**Common mistakes:**

- します → **する** ✅
- きます → **来る (くる)** ✅

## 2. ～ことができます

**Meaning / Use:** Used to say **can / be able to**.

**Structure:**

`V-dictionary + ことができます`

For a noun:

`N + が + できます`

**Explanation:**

- With a noun, use **N + ができます**.
- With a verb, use **V-dictionary + ことができます**.
- **こと** turns an action into an idea/noun.
- You do not need to translate **こと** every time.

**Examples:**

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|ミラーさんは日本語ができます。|Miraa-san wa Nihongo ga dekimasu.|Mr. Miller can speak Japanese.|
|スキーができます。|Sukii ga dekimasu.|I can ski.|
|ミラーさんは漢字を読むことができます。|Miraa-san wa kanji o yomu koto ga dekimasu.|Mr. Miller can read Kanji.|
|カードではらうことができます。|Kaado de harau koto ga dekimasu.|You can pay by credit card.|

**Common mistakes:**

- Use **V-dictionary + ことができます** for a verb.
- Do not use the ます-form before **ことができます**.

---

## 3. こと

**Meaning / Use:** **こと** turns an action into an idea/noun.

**Structure:**

`V-dictionary + こと`

**Explanation:**

- よむ → **よむこと**
- よむこと → the act of reading
- よむことができます → can read
- Remember: **V-dictionary + ことができます = can V**

**Examples:**

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|読むこと|yomu koto|the act of reading|
|漢字を読むことができます。|Kanji o yomu koto ga dekimasu.|I can read Kanji.|
|音楽を聞くことです。|Ongaku o kiku koto desu.|It is listening to music.|
|音楽を聞くこと|Ongaku o kiku koto|listening|

---

## 4. My Hobby ～ことです

**Meaning / Use:** Used to say what your hobby or activity is.

**Structure:**

`Watashi no shumi wa + N desu.`

or

`Watashi no shumi wa + V-dictionary + koto desu.`

**Explanation:**

- **趣味 (shumi)** = hobby.
- A noun can come after **趣味は**.
- A verb uses **V-dictionary + ことです**.
- **V-dictionary + ことです** means the activity of doing V.

**Examples:**

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|わたしの趣味は音楽です。|Watashi no shumi wa ongaku desu.|My hobby is music.|
|わたしの趣味は音楽を聞くことです。|Watashi no shumi wa ongaku o kiku koto desu.|My hobby is listening to music.|
|趣味は音楽を聞くことです。|Shumi wa ongaku o kiku koto desu.|My hobby is listening to music.|
|音楽を聞くことです。|Ongaku o kiku koto desu.|It is listening to music.|

**Common mistakes:**

- Use the **dictionary form** before **ことです**.

## 5. ～まえに

**Meaning / Use:** Used to say **before** doing something.

**Structure:**

`V-dictionary + まえに + V`

**Explanation:**

- The action after **まえに** happens first.
- The verb before **まえに** stays in dictionary form.
- This is true even when the whole sentence is past or future.
- **kuru mae ni** ✅, not **kimashita mae ni** ❌.

**Examples:**

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|日本へ来るまえに、日本語を勉強しました。|Nihon e kuru mae ni, Nihongo o benkyou shimashita.|I studied Japanese before I came to Japan.|
|寝るまえに、本を読みます。|Neru mae ni, hon o yomimasu.|I read a book before I go to bed.|
|来るまえに、日本語を勉強しました。|Kuru mae ni, Nihongo o benkyou shimashita.|I studied Japanese before coming.|
|寝るまえに、本を読みます。|Neru mae ni, hon o yomimasu.|I read a book before sleeping.|

**Common mistakes:**

- **kuru mae ni** ✅
- **kimashita mae ni** ❌

---

## 6. ～まえに with a Noun

**Meaning / Use:** Used to say **before a noun**.

**Structure:**

`N + の + まえに`

**Explanation:**

- A noun uses **の** before **まえに**.
- **N + no + mae ni** = before N.
- For verbs, use the dictionary form instead.

**Examples:**

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|食事のまえに、手を洗います。|Shokuji no mae ni, te o araimasu.|I wash my hands before eating.|
|食事のまえに、手を洗います。|Shokuji no mae ni, te o araimasu.|I wash my hands before the meal.|
|食事のまえに。|Shokuji no mae ni.|Before the meal.|
|手を洗います。|Te o araimasu.|I wash my hands.|

---

## 7. ～まえに with Time / Quantity

**Meaning / Use:** Used with a quantity or period to express **ago**.

**Structure:**

`Quantity / Period + まえに`

**Explanation:**

- **の** is not needed after a quantity or period.
- **いちじかんまえに** = an hour ago.
- Compare: **shokuji no mae ni** = before the meal.
- Compare: **ichi-jikan mae ni** = an hour ago.

**Examples:**

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|田中さんは一時間まえに出かけました。|Tanaka-san wa ichi-jikan mae ni dekakemashita.|Mr. Tanaka left an hour ago.|
|一時間まえに出かけました。|Ichi-jikan mae ni dekakemashita.|I left an hour ago.|
|食事のまえに。|Shokuji no mae ni.|Before the meal.|
|一時間まえに。|Ichi-jikan mae ni.|An hour ago.|

---

## 8. なかなか

**Meaning / Use:** With a negative expression, **なかなか** means **not easily / not as expected / rarely**.

**Structure:**

`なかなか + Negative`

**Explanation:**

- It is used with a negative expression.
- Think: **“It does not happen easily.”**
- The pattern is **nakanaka + negative**.

**Examples:**

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|日本ではなかなか馬を見ることができません。|Nihon de wa nakanaka uma o miru koto ga dekimasen.|In Japan, you cannot easily see horses.|
|なかなかできません。|Nakanaka dekimasen.|Cannot do easily.|
|日本ではなかなか馬を見ることができません。|Nihon de wa nakanaka uma o miru koto ga dekimasen.|In Japan, you cannot easily see horses.|
|なかなかできません。|Nakanaka dekimasen.|Cannot do easily.|

---

## 9. ぜひ

**Meaning / Use:** Used with hopes, requests, and strong desires. It means **definitely / by all means / I really want to**.

**Structure:**

`ぜひ + Sentence`

**Explanation:**

- **ぜひ** expresses a strong desire or request.
- It can be used with **～たいです**.
- It can be used with **～てください**.
- Remember: **zehi = definitely / by all means**.

**Examples:**

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|ぜひ北海道へ行きたいです。|Zehi Hokkaidou e ikitai desu.|I really want to go to Hokkaido.|
|ぜひ遊びに来てください。|Zehi asobi ni kite kudasai.|Please come visit me by all means.|
|ぜひ来てください。|Zehi kite kudasai.|Please come by all means.|
|ぜひ北海道へ行きたいです。|Zehi Hokkaidou e ikitai desu.|I really want to go to Hokkaido.|

## 10. ～ことができます vs ～ことです

**Meaning / Use:** These two patterns have different meanings.

**Structure:**

`V-dictionary + ことができます` → can V

`V-dictionary + ことです` → hobby / activity

**Explanation:**

- **dekimasu → CAN**
- **koto desu → IS THE ACTIVITY**
- Both patterns use the dictionary form before **こと**.
- **ことができます** expresses ability.

**Examples:**

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|漢字を読むことができます。|Kanji o yomu koto ga dekimasu.|I can read Kanji.|
|趣味は音楽を聞くことです。|Shumi wa ongaku o kiku koto desu.|My hobby is listening to music.|
|日本語を話すことができます。|Nihongo o hanasu koto ga dekimasu.|I can speak Japanese.|
|音楽を聞くことです。|Ongaku o kiku koto desu.|It is listening to music.|
