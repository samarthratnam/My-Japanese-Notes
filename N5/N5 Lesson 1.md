# Lesson 1 – Grammar

## 1. Positive Sentence

**N1 wa N2 desu.**

Meaning:

> **N1 is N2.**

Example:

> Watashi wa gakusei desu.  
> I am a student.

---

## 2. Negative Sentence

**N1 wa N2 ja arimasen.**

Meaning:

> **N1 is not N2.**

Example:

> Watashi wa sensei ja arimasen.  
> I am not a teacher.

---

## 3. Question Sentence

**N1 wa N2 desu ka?**

Meaning:

> Is N1 N2?

Example:

> Anata wa gakusei desu ka?  
> Are you a student?

Answer:

- **Hai, N1 wa N2 desu.** (Yes.)
- **Iie, N1 wa N2 ja arimasen.** (No.)

---

## 4. "Also"

**N1 mo N2 desu.**

Meaning:

> N1 is also N2.

Example:

> Watashi mo gakusei desu.  
> I am also a student.
