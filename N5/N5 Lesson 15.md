# SECTION 2: GRAMMAR

## 1. ～てもいいですか

### ～てもいいですか

**Meaning / Use:**  
Used to ask for permission.

**"May I do V?" / "Is it okay if I do V?"**

**Structure:**

> **V-te + も + いいですか**

**Explanation:**

- **V-te** = verb in て-form.
    
- **もいいですか** asks whether something is okay.
    
- Used when asking for permission.
    
- A positive answer can be **はい、どうぞ** or **ええ、どうぞ**.
    
- **いいえ、ちょっと......** is a softer refusal.
    

**Examples:**

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|ここで 写真を 撮っても いいですか。|Koko de shashin o totte mo ii desu ka?|May I take pictures here?|
|たばこを 吸っても いいですか。|Tabako o sutte mo ii desu ka?|May I smoke?|
|この カタログを もらっても いいですか。|Kono katarogu o moratte mo ii desu ka?|May I have this catalog?|
|ええ、どうぞ。|Ee, douzo.|Sure, go ahead.|
|はい、どうぞ。|Hai, douzo.|Yes, please.|

**Common mistakes:**  
Do not confuse **～てもいいですか** with prohibition. It asks for permission.

---

## 2. ～てはいけません

### ～てはいけません

**Meaning / Use:**  
Used when something is not allowed or prohibited.

**"You must not do V."**

**Structure:**

> **V-te + は + いけません**

**Explanation:**

- **V-te** = verb in て-form.
    
- **はいけません** means something is not allowed.
    
- Used for prohibition.
    
- It is the opposite idea of **～てもいいですか**.
    

**Examples:**

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|ここで たばこを 吸っては いけません。|Koko de tabako o sutte wa ikemasen.|You must not smoke here.|
|ここで たばこを 吸っては いけません。|Koko de tabako o sutte wa ikemasen.|Smoking is not allowed here.|
|V-te + wa ikemasen.|V-te wa ikemasen.|You must not do V.|
|V-te + mo ii desu ka?|V-te mo ii desu ka?|May I do V?|

**Common mistakes:**

- **V-te mo ii desu ka?** → May I do it?
    
- **V-te wa ikemasen.** → You must not do it.

## 3. ～ています — State / Continuing Condition

### ～ています

**Meaning / Use:**  
Used to describe a continuing state or condition.

It can mean **am married, know someone, live somewhere, have something**, etc.

**Structure:**

> **V-te + imasu**

**Explanation:**

- **～ています** does not always mean **"am doing."**
    
- It can describe a continuing state.
    
- It can describe knowing someone.
    
- It can describe living somewhere or possessing something.
    
- Look at the meaning of the verb to understand the state.
    

**Examples:**

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|わたしは 結婚しています。|Watashi wa kekkon shite imasu.|I am married.|
|わたしは 田中さんを 知っています。|Watashi wa Tanaka-san o shitte imasu.|I know Mr. Tanaka.|
|わたしは 大阪に 住んでいます。|Watashi wa Oosaka ni sunde imasu.|I live in Osaka.|
|わたしは カメラを 持っています。|Watashi wa kamera o motte imasu.|I have a camera.|

**Common mistakes:**  
Do not automatically translate every **V-te imasu** as **"am doing."** In this chapter it can describe a continuing state.

## 4. ～ています — Regular / Habitual Actions

### ～ています

**Meaning / Use:**  
Used to describe an action that someone regularly does.

It can describe someone's regular work, study, production, or selling activity.

**Structure:**

> **Noun + は + Place + で + V-te + imasu**

**Explanation:**

- **～ています** can describe a regular activity.
    
- **で** can show the place where the activity happens.
    
- The action is presented as a regular activity.
    
- The same **V-te imasu** pattern can describe either a state or a regular activity.
    

**Examples:**

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|IMCは コンピューターソフトを 作っています。|IMC wa konpyuutaa sofuto o tsukutte imasu.|IMC makes computer software.|
|スーパーは フィルムを 売っています。|Suupaa wa firumu o utte imasu.|Supermarkets sell film.|
|ミラーさんは LMCで 働いています。|Miraa-san wa LMC de hataraite imasu.|Mr. Miller works at LMC.|
|姉は 大学で 勉強しています。|Ane wa daigaku de benkyou shite imasu.|My older sister studies at university.|

**Common mistakes:**  
Do not assume **～ています** always means an action happening right now. It can also describe a regular activity.

## 5. 知りません

### 知りません

**Meaning / Use:**  
Used to say **"I don't know."**

The chapter uses **知っています** for the state of knowing and **知りません** for not knowing.

**Structure:**

> **Noun + を + 知っていますか**

Answer:

> **はい、知っています。**

or

> **いいえ、知りません。**

**Explanation:**

- **知っています** = know.
    
- **知りません** = don't know.
    
- **しる** means to get to know / learn.
    
- For the state of knowing, **知っています** is used.
    
- The negative expression is **知りません**.
    

**Examples:**

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|市役所の 電話番号を 知っていますか。|Shiyakusho no denwa bangou o shitte imasu ka?|Do you know the telephone number of the city hall?|
|はい、知っています。|Hai, shitte imasu.|Yes, I know.|
|いいえ、知りません。|Iie, shirimasen.|No, I don't know.|
|田中さんを 知っています。|Tanaka-san o shitte imasu.|I know Mr. Tanaka.|
|田中さんを 知りません。|Tanaka-san o shirimasen.|I don't know Mr. Tanaka.|

**Common mistakes:**  
Remember:

> **知っています = know**  
> **知りません = don't know**

# 6. ～ています — Three Useful Meanings

### ～ています

**Meaning / Use:**  
In Chapter 15, **～ています** is used for both continuing states and regular activities.

**Structure:**

> **V-te + imasu**

**Explanation:**

- **結婚しています** → am married
    
- **知っています** → know
    
- **住んでいます** → live / reside
    
- **持っています** → have / possess
    
- It can also describe regular activities such as work, study, making, and selling.
    

**Examples:**

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|結婚しています。|kekkon shite imasu.|am married|
|知っています。|shitte imasu.|know|
|住んでいます。|sunde imasu.|live / reside|
|持っています。|motte imasu.|have / possess|
|働いています。|hataraite imasu.|work|
|勉強しています。|benkyou shite imasu.|study|
|作っています。|tsukutte imasu.|make / produce|
|売っています。|utte imasu.|sell|

**Common mistakes:**  
When you see **V-te imasu**, ask whether it describes a **continuing state** or a **regular activity**.

# 7. Chapter 15 Important Patterns

## Pattern 1 — Permission

> **V-te mo ii desu ka?**

**Meaning:** May I do V?

**Example:**

> Shashin o totte mo ii desu ka?  
> May I take a picture?

---

## Pattern 2 — Prohibition

> **V-te wa ikemasen.**

**Meaning:** You must not do V.

**Example:**

> Koko de tabako o sutte wa ikemasen.  
> You must not smoke here.

---

## Pattern 3 — Continuing State

> **V-te imasu.**

**Meaning:** I am / have / know / live...

**Example:**

> Watashi wa Oosaka ni sunde imasu.  
> I live in Osaka.

---

## Pattern 4 — Regular Activity

> **V-te imasu.**

**Meaning:** Someone regularly does V.

**Example:**

> Ane wa daigaku de benkyou shite imasu.  
> My older sister studies at university.
