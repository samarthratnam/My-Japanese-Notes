# 4. Counters for Flat/Thin Objects — ～枚

Your notes mention:

> **～mai → counters for thin, flat objects such as paper, stamps, etc.**

Correct. 👍

Use **枚 (mai)** for things such as:

- paper
- stamps
- postcards
- shirts
- plates
- tickets

### Examples

> 紙を三枚買いました。  
> **Kami o san-mai kaimashita.**  
> I bought three sheets of paper.

> 切手を二枚ください。  
> **Kitte o ni-mai kudasai.**  
> Please give me two stamps.

# 5. ～回 — Number of Times

**～回 (kai)** = number of times

Examples:

- 一回 — ikkai → once
- 二回 — nikai → twice
- 三回 — sankai → three times
- 四回 — yonkai → four times
- 五回 — gokai → five times

### Example

> 一週間に三回運動します。  
> **Isshuukan ni sankai undou shimasu.**  
> I exercise three times a week.

# 7. Important Counter Pronunciations

### ～日 — days

This one has several irregular readings.

|Number|Japanese|Romaji|
|---|---|---|
|1 day|一日|tsuitachi|
|2 days|二日|futsuka|
|3 days|三日|mikka|
|4 days|四日|yokka|

|7 days|七日|nanoka|

> A: 行ってまいります。

> B: 行ってらっしゃい。  
> _Itterasshai._  
> See you later.

# II. Quantifier Placement

Your note says:

> **N + quantifier + verb**

This is the key pattern.

## Pattern

```
N + を + Quantity + Verb
```

### Example

> りんごを五つ買いました。  
> **Ringo o itsutsu kaimashita.**

I bought five apples.

Breakdown:

```
りんごを → apples
五つ      → five
買いました → bought
```

---

## People

For people, use **～人**.

> 家族が四人います。  
> **Kazoku ga yo-nin imasu.**

There are four people in my family.

### ⚠️ Important

1 person = **一人 (hitori)**  
2 people = **二人 (futari)**

Then:

3 people = 三人 **san-nin**  
4 people = 四人 **yo-nin**

---

# III. Time Period + Verb

This is one of the most important parts of Lesson 11.

## Pattern

```
Subject + Time Period + Verb
```

The time period tells us **how long the action lasted**.

---

## Example from your notes

### I studied Japanese for three months.

> 私は三か月日本語を勉強しました。  
> **Watashi wa san-kagetsu Nihongo o benkyou shimashita.**

I studied Japanese for three months.

### Breakdown

```
私は       → I
三か月     → three months
日本語を   → Japanese
勉強しました → studied
```

---

## Example 2

> スペインへ二週間旅行しました。  
> **Supein e ni-shuukan ryokou shimashita.**

I traveled to Spain for two weeks.

---

## Example 3

> クリスマスで一年働きました。 ❌

Your intended sentence is:

> クリスマスで一年働きました。

But if you mean **"I worked at [a place] for one year,"** you need the place:

> 会社で一年働きました。  
> **Kaisha de ichi-nen hatarakimashita.**

I worked at a company for one year.

### ⭐ Rule

The time period normally does **not** need に here.

> 三か月勉強しました。 ✅  
> I studied for three months.

Not:

> 三か月に勉強しました。 ❌

# ⭐ VERY IMPORTANT: だけ vs しか

You haven't written **しか** here, but it is useful to know the difference.

### だけ

> 一つだけあります。  
> There is only one.

### しか + negative

> 一つしかありません。  
> There is only one.  
> _(literally: there isn't anything except one)_

For now, focus on **だけ**.

# 🔥 Lesson 11 — The Most Important Patterns

## 1. How many objects?

```
N を + Quantity + Verb
```

> りんごを三つ買いました。  
> I bought three apples.

---

## 2. How many people?

```
N が + Number of people + います
```

> 家族が四人います。  
> There are four people in my family.

---

## 3. How long?

```
Time period + Verb
```

> 三か月日本語を勉強しました。  
> I studied Japanese for three months.

---

## 4. How many times during a period?

```
Time period + に + Frequency + Verb
```

> 一週間に三回勉強します。  
> I study three times a week.

---

## 5. Only

```
Quantity + だけ + Verb
```

> 一人だけ来ました。  
> Only one person came.

---

## 6. How long does it take?

```
Time + かかります
```

> 三時間かかります。  
> It takes three hours.
