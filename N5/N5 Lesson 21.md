# Section 2: Grammar

## ① Plain Form + と思います

### ～と思います

**Meaning / Use:**  
Used to say **“I think...”** or give your opinion.

**Structure:**

> Plain form + と + 思います

**Explanation:**

- `と` marks the idea/thought.
    
- The sentence before `と` uses the **plain form**.
    
- The inside sentence can be positive or negative.
    
- `と思います` means “I think”.
    

**Examples:**

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|明日 雨が降ると思います。|Ashita ame ga furu to omoimasu.|I think it will rain tomorrow.|
|日本は物価が高いと思います。|Nihon wa bukka ga takai to omoimasu.|I think prices are high in Japan.|
|きれいだと思います。|Kirei da to omoimasu.|I think it is clean.|
|忙しいと思います。|Isogashii to omoimasu.|I think it is busy.|

**Common mistakes:**  
Do not use the polite form directly before `と`. Use the **plain form**.

---

## ② Negative Opinion / Guess

### Negative plain form + と思います

**Meaning / Use:**  
Used to say **“I don't think...”** when the idea inside the sentence is negative.

**Structure:**

> Negative plain form + と + 思います

**Explanation:**

- The negative goes **before** `と`.
    
- `知らない` = don't know.
    
- `知らないと思います` = I think [he/she] doesn't know.
    
- `と思います` itself is not made negative for this meaning.
    

**Examples:**

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|たぶん知らないと思います。|Tabun shiranai to omoimasu.|I don't think he/she knows.|
|そんなに寒くなかったと思います。|Sonnani samukunakatta to omoimasu.|I don't think it was that cold.|
|行かないと思います。|Ikanai to omoimasu.|I don't think [someone] will go.|
|来ないと思います。|Konai to omoimasu.|I don't think [someone] will come.|

**Common mistakes:**  
❌ `shiranai omoimasu`  
✅ `shiranai to omoimasu`

## ③ ～についてどう思いますか

### ～についてどう思いますか

**Meaning / Use:**  
Used to ask **“What do you think about ~?”**

**Structure:**

> N + について + どう思いますか

**Explanation:**

- `について` means **about / concerning**.
    
- `どう` means **how / what** in this question.
    
- `思いますか` asks for an opinion.
    
- The answer can use `～と思います`.
    

**Examples:**

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|新しい空港についてどう思いますか。|Atarashii kuukou ni tsuite dou omoimasu ka?|What do you think about the new airport?|
|日本の政治についてどう思いますか。|Nihon no seiji ni tsuite dou omoimasu ka?|What do you think about Japanese politics?|
|このニュースについてどう思いますか。|Kono nyuusu ni tsuite dou omoimasu ka?|What do you think about this news?|
|交通についてどう思いますか。|Koutsuu ni tsuite dou omoimasu ka?|What do you think about transportation/traffic?|

**Common mistakes:**  
Use `について` before the topic you are asking about.

## ④ Agreeing / Disagreeing

### そう思います / そうは思いません

**Meaning / Use:**  
Used to agree or disagree with an opinion.

**Structure:**

> そう + 思います  
> そう + は + 思いません

**Explanation:**

- `そう思います` = I think so.
    
- `そうは思いません` = I don't think so.
    
- `は` adds emphasis to the negative.
    
- `私もそう思います` = I think so too.
    

**Examples:**

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|私もそう思います。|Watashi mo sou omoimasu.|I think so too.|
|私はそうは思いません。|Watashi wa sou wa omoimasen.|I don't think so.|
|便利です。私もそう思います。|Benri desu. Watashi mo sou omoimasu.|It is convenient. I think so too.|
|私はそうは思いません。|Watashi wa sou wa omoimasen.|I don't think so.|

## ⑤ Plain Form + と言います

### ～と言います

**Meaning / Use:**  
Used to say or quote what someone says.

**Structure:**

> Plain form + と + 言います

**Explanation:**

- `と` marks what is said.
    
- Indirect speech uses the **plain form**.
    
- `言います` = say.
    
- `言いました` = said.
    

**Examples:**

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|雨が降ると言いました。|Ame ga furu to iimashita.|He/she said it will rain.|
|来ると言いました。|Kuru to iimashita.|He/she said he/she would come.|
|東京へ出張すると言いました。|Tokyo e shucchou suru to iimashita.|He/she said he/she would go to Tokyo on a business trip.|
|ミラーさんは来週東京へ出張すると言いました。|Miraa-san wa raishuu Tokyo e shucchou suru to iimashita.|Mr. Miller said he would go to Tokyo on a business trip next week.|

**Common mistakes:**  
Do not automatically change the quoted sentence to past tense just because `言いました` is past.

---

## ⑥ Direct Quotation

### 「...」と言います

**Meaning / Use:**  
Used when quoting someone's **exact words**.

**Structure:**

> 「Exact words」+ と + 言います

**Explanation:**

- The exact words go inside `「 」`.
    
- `と` marks the quotation.
    
- `言います` means “say”.
    
- `言いました` means “said”.
    

**Examples:**

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|「おやすみなさい」と言います。|"Oyasuminasai" to iimasu.|We say “Good night.”|
|ミラーさんは「来週東京へ出張します」と言いました。|Miraa-san wa "Raishuu Tokyo e shucchou shimasu" to iimashita.|Mr. Miller said, “I will go to Tokyo on a business trip next week.”|
|「もちろん」と言いました。|"Mochiron" to iimashita.|He/she said, “Of course.”|
|「行きます」と言いました。|"Ikimasu" to iimashita.|He/she said, “I will go.”|

## ⑦ ～でしょう？

### ～でしょう？

**Meaning / Use:**  
Used to ask for confirmation or agreement: **“..., right?” / “..., isn't it?”**

**Structure:**

> Plain form + でしょう？

**Explanation:**

- Verbs use the plain form.
    
- い-adjectives use the plain form.
    
- な-adjectives use the plain form.
    
- Nouns use the noun + `だ` pattern in plain form.
    
- In this lesson, `でしょう？` is mainly used to confirm agreement.
    

**Examples:**

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|明日パーティーに行くでしょう？|Ashita paatii ni iku deshou?|You're going to the party tomorrow, aren't you?|
|北海道は寒かったでしょう？|Hokkaidou wa samukatta deshou?|It was cold in Hokkaido, wasn't it?|
|この町は静かでしょう？|Kono machi wa shizuka deshou?|This town is quiet, isn't it?|
|田中さんは学生でしょう？|Tanaka-san wa gakusei deshou?|Tanaka is a student, right?|

**Common mistakes:**  
Remember:

> Verb → `iku deshou?`  
> い-adjective → `samui deshou?`  
> な-adjective → `shizuka deshou?`  
> Noun → `gakusei deshou?`

## ⑧ N(place) で N が あります

### Place で Event が あります

**Meaning / Use:**  
Used when an **event takes place / is held** at a place.

**Structure:**

> Place + で + Event + が + あります

**Explanation:**

- `で` marks the place where the event happens.
    
- `が` marks the event.
    
- With events, `あります` can mean **take place / be held**.
    
- Here `あります` does not simply mean “there is”.
    

**Examples:**

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|東京で試合があります。|Tokyo de shiai ga arimasu.|A game will be held in Tokyo.|
|京都でお祭りがあります。|Kyouto de omatsuri ga arimasu.|A festival will be held in Kyoto.|
|学校でパーティーがあります。|Gakkou de paatii ga arimasu.|A party will be held at school.|
|東京で日本とブラジルのサッカーの試合があります。|Tokyo de Nihon to Burajiru no sakkaa no shiai ga arimasu.|A soccer match between Japan and Brazil will be held in Tokyo.|

## ⑨ N(Occasion) で

### Nで

**Meaning / Use:**  
Used for **“at / on the occasion of N”**.

**Structure:**

> N + で + Action

**Explanation:**

- `で` marks the occasion/place.
    
- The noun can be an occasion such as a meeting.
    
- `会議で` = at the meeting.
    
- The action happens at that occasion.
    

**Examples:**

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|会議で何か意見を言いましたか。|Kaigi de nanika iken o iimashita ka?|Did you give your opinion at the meeting?|
|会議で意見を言いました。|Kaigi de iken o iimashita.|I gave my opinion at the meeting.|
|学校で話しました。|Gakkou de hanashimashita.|I talked at school.|
|パーティーで飲みました。|Paatii de nomimashita.|I drank at the party.|

## ⑩ Nでも V

### NでもV

**Meaning / Use:**  
Used to give an example or make a suggestion: **“N or something.”**

**Structure:**

> N + でも + V

**Explanation:**

- `でも` gives N as an example.
    
- It does not mean that N is the only choice.
    
- It is often used for suggestions.
    
- `Nでも...` = N or something / something like N.
    

**Examples:**

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|ちょっとビールでも飲みませんか。|Chotto biiru demo nomimasen ka?|Shall we have a beer or something?|
|コーヒーでも飲みませんか。|Koohii demo nomimasen ka?|Shall we have some coffee or something?|
|映画でも見ませんか。|Eiga demo mimasen ka?|Shall we watch a movie or something?|
|ちょっと休んでもどうですか。|Chotto yasundemo dou desu ka?|How about taking a little rest?|

## ⑪ Vないと……

### Vないと……

**Meaning / Use:**  
A shortened casual expression meaning **“I've got to...” / “I have to...”**

**Structure:**

> V-nai + と……

**Explanation:**

- It comes from `V-nai to ikemasen`.
    
- It can also come from `V-nakereba narimasen`.
    
- The ending is omitted because the meaning is understood.
    
- In this short pattern, `V-nai to...` means **“I've got to V...”**.
    

**Examples:**

|Japanese|Romaji|English|
|---|---|---|
|もう帰らないと……|Mou kaeranai to...|I've got to go home now...|
|行かないと……|Ikanai to...|I've got to go...|
|勉強しないと……|Benkyou shinai to...|I've got to study...|
|見ないと……|Minai to...|I've got to see / I must see...|

**Common mistakes:**  
Do not interpret this shortened pattern simply as “don't”. Here `Vないと...` means **“I've got to V...”**.

# Compare Similar Patterns

### ～と思います

**Samui to omoimasu.**  
→ I think it is cold.

**Use:** giving your opinion.

### ～でしょう？

**Samui deshou?**  
→ It is cold, isn't it?

**Use:** asking the listener to agree or confirm.

### ～と思います vs ～と言います

|Pattern|Meaning|
|---|---|
|～と思います|I think...|
|～と言います|say that...|

**Ame ga furu to omoimasu.**  
→ I think it will rain.

**Ame ga furu to iimashita.**  
→ He/she said it will rain.

# Important Negative Patterns

### I don't think...

> Negative plain form + と + 思います

**Shiranai to omoimasu.**  
→ I don't think [he/she] knows.

### Have to...

> V-nai to...

**Kaeranai to...**  
→ I've got to go home...

### Lesson 20 inside Lesson 21

**Itta koto ga nai.**  
→ I have never been.

**Itta koto ga nai to omoimasu.**  
→ I think [he/she] has never been.
